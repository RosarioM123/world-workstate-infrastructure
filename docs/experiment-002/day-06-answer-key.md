## Decisions (D1-D11)

- D1: Phase 1 is research only; no valuation modeling, no buy/hold/sell recommendation.
- D2: Treat the streaming-margin inflection as the core 3-5 year profit thesis
  (FY2022 ~$4B annual streaming losses to 10%+ operating margins FY2026).
- D3a: Model domestic parks as attendance-led volume growth. Status: SUPERSEDED by D3b.
- D3b: Treat domestic parks growth as pricing/per-cap-spend led, attendance flat to
  modest. Supersedes D3a. Evidence: Q2 FY2026 attendance -1% with OI +5%;
  Q3 FY2026 per-cap spend +4-5%.
- D4: ESPN DTC as strategic option with unproven net economics; do not assume it
  offsets linear decline one-for-one. Linear networks OI fell 21% YoY fiscal Q4 2025.
- D5: Exclude Abu Dhabi from capex modeling; model only royalty revenue
  (zero-capex Miral license structure).
- D6: Carry the WBD acquisition as unresolved competitive overhang; no point forecast
  (E9/E10 source conflict).
- D7: Carry the Epic Games stake as option value, not a thesis pillar. Depends on D2.
- D8: Treat dividend ($1.50/share) plus raised buyback (to at least $9B FY2026) as
  downside support, not a growth driver.
- D9: Succession reduces execution risk vs external hire; Iger as senior advisor
  through Dec 31, 2026 is residual transition risk.
- D10: Constrain franchise reliability: Star Wars / live-action remakes are not
  automatic theatrical hits. Depends on D3b.
- D11: Use FY2026 management guidance (double-digit adj. EPS growth FY26/FY27,
  $19B operating cash flow) as public-anchor baseline, not fact.
