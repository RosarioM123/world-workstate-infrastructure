# Agent A Final Report - The Walt Disney Company (NYSE: DIS), 3-5 Year Horizon

Phase 1 research (Agent A). Valuation modeling and buy/hold/sell recommendation are explicitly out of scope; those are Phase 2 work.

## Findings (with sources)

**Streaming economics.** Disney+ / Hulu / ESPN DTC together reached sustained profitability. Fiscal Q2 2026 (quarter ended March 28): revenue $5.49B (+13%), operating income $582M (+88% YoY), first quarter above 10% operating margin (10.6%). Fiscal Q3 2026 (quarter ended June 27): streaming OI more than doubled to $712M, 13% margin. Management targets at least 10% streaming operating margin for full FY2026, up from ~5% in FY2025. Disney stopped disclosing specific subscriber counts (shielding from churn volatility), so sub growth must be inferred from revenue/ARPU lines. ## Assumptions (with explicit confidence)

1. Streaming margin trajectory continues upward (FY2026 target 10%, from ~5% in FY2025). Confidence: high. Basis: three consecutive quarters of margin expansion.
2. Parks growth is driven by per-cap spend and pricing, not attendance volume, over the 3-5y horizon. Confidence: high. Basis: Q2 FY2026 attendance -1% with OI +5%; Q3 FY2026 attendance +3% with per-cap spend +4-5%.
3. Domestic linear networks decline continues at a similar pace (double-digit annual OI decline). Confidence: medium. Basis: -21% YoY in fiscal Q4 2025, but sports rights renegotiations could change the slope.
4. ESPN DTC is net positive to segment economics once past launch investment. Confidence: medium. Basis: management success claims, bundle uptake; affiliate-fee cannibalization unquantified.
5. Star Wars and live-action remakes cannot be assumed automatic theatrical hits. Confidence: high. Basis: Mandalorian & Grogu lowest-grossing live-action Star Wars; Moana remake loss-making theatrically against $250M budget.
6. D'Amaro-led Disney continues Iger's four strategic priorities (studio quality, streaming profitability, ESPN digital, Experiences growth) with no sharp pivot. Confidence: medium. Basis: continuity team (Walden, Bergman, Pitaro retained); no new published strategy found.
7. The Abu Dhabi resort requires no Disney capex (pure royalty model). Confidence: high. Basis: Disney's own press release and Iger's Q2 2025 earnings call statements.
8. The Epic Games universe ships within the 3-5y horizon and becomes a material engagement channel. Confidence: low. Basis: announced Feb 2024, no launch date, no progress updates found.
9. Debt remains investment-grade and declining; no refinancing stress. Confidence: medium. Basis: third-party research note only (net debt/EBITDA 1.9x); not verified from filings.
10. The buyback program ($7B raised to at least $9B for FY2026) is executed as announced. Confidence: medium. Basis: management history of executing buybacks, but FY2026 completion not yet reported.

## Revisions (old -> new -> triggering evidence)

- R1: Streaming is a drag on earnings -> Streaming is a sustained profit engine with 10%+ margins. Trigger: Q2 and Q3 FY2026 streaming OI ($582M +88%, then $712M, 13% margin), vs FY2022 ~$4B annual losses.
- R2: Iger remains CEO with unresolved succession -> D'Amaro named CEO effective March 18, 2026; succession resolved with continuity team. Trigger: Feb 3, 2026 Disney board announcement; D'Amaro's May 2026 earnings debut.
- R3: Epic Universe will take meaningful share from Disney World -> Epic Universe is real but Disney's attendance is holding; growth is pricing-led. Trigger: Disney fiscal Q3 attendance +3% global; MoffettNathanson ~1M-visitor impact estimate; Disney CFO comments of minimal impact.

## Decisions (with rationale)

- D1: Phase 1 is research only; no valuation, no recommendation. Rationale: protocol scope boundary; prevents premature conclusions before Phase 2 modeling.
- D2: Treat the streaming-margin inflection as the core 3-5y profit thesis. Rationale: it is the largest single P&L change vs the 2022-23 base case.
- D3a (superseded): Model domestic parks as attendance-led volume growth. Rationale at time: historical pattern. Superseded by D3b after Q2/Q3 FY2026 data showed pricing-led growth.
- D3b: Treat domestic parks growth as pricing/per-cap-spend led, attendance flat to modest. Rationale: three quarters of data showing OI growth on flat-to-down attendance.
- D4: Treat ESPN DTC as a strategic option with unproven net economics; do not assume it offsets linear decline one-for-one. Rationale: launch bundle pricing ($29.99) and cannibalization are undisclosed.
- D5: Exclude Abu Dhabi from capex modeling; model only royalty revenue. Rationale: Disney's own disclosure of zero-capex license structure.
- D6: Carry the Netflix-vs-Paramount-Skydance WBD deal as an unresolved competitive overhang; no point forecast. Rationale: source conflict (Dec 2025 Netflix deal vs Feb 2026 Paramount Skydance board recommendation); honesty over precision.
- D7: Carry the Epic Games stake as option value, not as a thesis pillar. Rationale: no launch date, no metrics; depends on D2's streaming-as-engine framing (gaming extends engagement).
- D8: Treat dividend ($1.50) plus raised buyback (to at least $9B) as shareholder-return discipline that supports downside, not as a growth driver. Rationale: capital-return programs reflect maturity, not expansion.
- D9: Score the succession as reducing execution risk vs an external hire, but flag Iger's continued presence through Dec 2026 as residual transition risk. Rationale: unanimous board vote, continuity team, but Iger remains senior advisor.
- D10: Constrain franchise reliability: do not assume Star Wars or live-action remakes are automatic theatrical hits in any Phase 2 model. Rationale: Mandalorian & Grogu and Moana data. Depends on D3b in that it mirrors D3b's pattern of evidence-overriding-prior on franchise strength.
- D11: Use FY2026 management guidance (double-digit adjusted EPS growth FY26/FY27, $19B operating cash flow) as the public-anchor baseline, not as fact. Rationale: it is the market's reference point; testing it is Phase 2's job.

## Constraints and guardrails binding Phase 2

- G1: Do not build a buy/hold/sell recommendation on top of third-party research notes alone; verify debt, segment OI, and cash flow from Disney's own 10-K/10-Q before modeling.
- G2: Do not impute per-service subscriber counts; Disney stopped disclosing them. Work from revenue, OI, and ARPU lines only.
- G3: Conflicting claims (notably the WBD acquisition status) must be disclosed as conflicts, never averaged or silently picked.
