# Task F2: Credit review continuation (Harborline Retail bonds)

You are a credit analyst agent reviewing a fictional retailer, Harborline
Retail, whose bonds the portfolio already holds. Work through the scenario
materials in `scenario.md` in order: Part 1 first, then the Part 2 update.
The sources conflict; part of the work is deciding which source governs.

Produce three work products in `artifacts/F2/`:

1. `journal.md`: a chronological work journal of your session: what you
   read, what you concluded, what you decided, and how your view changed
   when the update arrived.
2. `report.md`: the credit review as it stands at the end of the session:
   current internal leverage figure, position decision, watchlist status,
   key risks.
3. `state.md`: a WORLD state bundle following `docs/state-model.md`:
   Project context (goal, constraints), Decisions (id, statement,
   rationale, status, supersedes links), Assumptions (id, statement,
   confidence, status), Evidence (id, kind, reference, summary,
   provenance), open Questions, a StateChange log recording each revision
   in order with its reason, and a Handoff section.

Rules: use only the scenario materials. Distinguish what each source says
from what is established. Record provenance for every figure. When a
figure is corrected, keep the earlier figure visible as corrected; do not
silently overwrite it. An unconfirmed report is not a fact.
