# Task R2: Onboarding user study plan

You are a research agent planning a moderated user study of a new
onboarding flow. Work through the scenario materials in `scenario.md` in
order: Part 1 is the original plan and an older instruction; Part 2
contains updates that reverse a decision, correct an assumption, and
supersede the older instruction. Track every version and which instruction
is authoritative.

Produce three work products in `artifacts/R2/`:

1. `journal.md`: a chronological work journal: the plan as drafted, what
   the updates changed, and your reasoning.
2. `report.md`: the study plan as it stands at the end of the session:
   format, session length, incentive, recruitment status, constraints.
3. `state.md`: a WORLD state bundle following `docs/state-model.md`:
   Project context (goal, constraints), Decisions (id, statement,
   rationale, status, supersedes links), Assumptions (id, statement,
   confidence, status), Evidence (id, kind, reference, summary,
   provenance), open Questions, a StateChange log recording each revision
   in order with its reason, and a Handoff section.

Rules: use only the scenario materials. Where two instructions conflict,
the later one from the instruction's owner governs; keep the superseded
instruction visible with its provenance. Label estimates as assumptions
with confidence, and record corrections to them.
