# Continuation assignment (C1)

You are resuming planning of a two-day team offsite that a previous
operations agent started. Using only the handoff material provided,
produce a Continuation Document with exactly these sections:

1. **Where things stand:** the current state of the plan: venue status,
   group size, and the budget picture using only figures that actually
   exist in the material.
2. **Decisions in force:** every decision made so far, with reasons.
   Separately list anything no longer in force.
3. **Assumptions and facts:** the assumptions in play, labeled as
   assumptions, with confidence. Keep given facts distinct from
   assumptions.
4. **Constraints:** every hard constraint on the offsite.
5. **What changed and why:** anything that changed during the prior
   session; if nothing changed, say so.
6. **Open questions and missing information:** everything still unknown,
   unresolved, or conflicting. Missing figures must be flagged as
   missing; do not estimate or invent them. Conflicts must be surfaced,
   not silently resolved.
7. **Next actions:** the concrete next steps, in order, within the
   constraints.
8. **Planning memo:** a short memo (at most about 300 words) to the team
   lead: budget table using only given figures and showing the total of
   known costs, what is still missing before a final booking, and the
   unresolved conflict that needs the venue to settle.
