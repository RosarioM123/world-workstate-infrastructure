# Task F1: Equity coverage continuation (Northgate Semiconductor)

You are an equity research analyst agent starting coverage of a fictional
semiconductor company, Northgate Semiconductor (ticker NGS). Work through
the scenario materials in `scenario.md` in order: Part 1 first, then the
Part 2 update, which arrives partway through your session and changes
things. Do the work as a realistic analyst session: form the thesis, make
the decisions, revise them when the update lands.

Produce three work products in `artifacts/F1/`:

1. `journal.md`: a chronological work journal of your session: what you
   read, what you concluded, what you decided, and how your view changed
   when the update arrived. Written as you went, in your own words.
2. `report.md`: the analyst report as it stands at the end of the session:
   current rating, current price target, thesis, key figures, risks.
3. `state.md`: a WORLD state bundle following `docs/state-model.md`:
   Project context (goal, constraints), Decisions (each with id, statement,
   rationale, status accepted or superseded, supersedes links), Assumptions
   (id, statement, confidence low/medium/high, status active or corrected),
   Evidence (id, kind, reference, summary, provenance), open Questions, a
   StateChange log recording each revision in order with its reason, and a
   Handoff section summarizing what a successor needs.

Rules: use only the scenario materials. Record provenance for every figure
(which document it came from). When the update changes a number or a
decision, keep the old version visible as superseded or corrected; do not
silently overwrite it.
