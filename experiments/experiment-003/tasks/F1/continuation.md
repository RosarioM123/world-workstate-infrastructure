# Continuation assignment (F1)

You are resuming equity coverage of Northgate Semiconductor (NGS) that a
previous analyst agent started. Using only the handoff material provided,
produce a Continuation Document with exactly these sections:

1. **Where things stand:** the current rating, current price target, and
   the current key figures for the thesis, as they stand now.
2. **Decisions in force:** every decision that currently governs this
   coverage, with the reason it was made. Separately list any decisions
   that are no longer in force.
3. **Assumptions and facts:** the assumptions the thesis rests on, labeled
   as assumptions, with how confident the prior work was in each. Keep
   established facts distinct from assumptions.
4. **Constraints:** every standing constraint that binds what you may
   recommend or publish.
5. **What changed and why:** everything that changed during the prior
   session (figures, estimates, decisions), what it changed from and to,
   why it changed, which version is authoritative now, and where the change
   came from.
6. **Open questions and missing information:** what is still unknown or
   undisclosed. Flag missing information; do not fill it in.
7. **Next actions:** the concrete next steps, in order. Do not propose
   redoing work the prior session already completed.
8. **Draft client note:** a short updated client note (at most about 300
   words) announcing the current rating and target, what changed and why,
   the key assumptions, and the main risk, following the constraints in
   section 4.
