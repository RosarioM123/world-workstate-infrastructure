# F1 scenario materials

## Part 1: initial materials (start of session)

### Company Q2 earnings release (primary source)
Northgate Semiconductor Q2 results: revenue $412M, up 19% year over year.
Gross margin 41.2%. Management guides full-year revenue growth of 18% and
full-year gross margin of approximately 41%, citing strong data-center
demand.

### Distributor channel check (call notes, your firm's channel contact)
A major distributor reports data-center orders steady through the quarter,
no cancellations, lead times stable. The contact describes end demand as
"strong and boring".

### Competitor sell-side note (secondary source, unverified)
A competitor firm's note claims Northgate is losing share in AI
accelerator sockets to a rival and that "channel checks are turning".
No primary data is cited in the note. Your firm's policy: competitor notes
are not a citable source for client-facing claims.

### Portfolio and compliance constraints (standing)
- Single-name position limit: 3% of portfolio.
- Client-facing factual claims must cite primary sources (company releases
  and filings), never competitor notes.
- Any published price target must be accompanied by the export-control
  risk disclosure section.

### Your working estimates at initiation
FY+1 EPS estimate $3.82. Valuation convention for this coverage: 22x FY+1
EPS.

## Part 2: update (arrives mid-session)

### Company press release: guidance revision (primary source)
Northgate revises full-year guidance: revenue growth now expected at 11%
(previously 18%) and gross margin now guided to approximately 38.5%,
citing new export-control restrictions affecting shipments to two
countries. The release does not specify which product lines or SKUs are
affected.

### Your revised estimates after the update
FY+1 EPS estimate revised to $3.05 on the new guidance. Valuation multiple
unchanged at 22x.
