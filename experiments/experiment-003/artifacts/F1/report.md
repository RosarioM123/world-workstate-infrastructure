# Northgate Semiconductor (NGS): Initiation, Revised on Guidance Cut

## Rating and price target

- Rating: Hold
- Price target: $67.10
- Basis for price target: FY+1 EPS estimate of $3.05 multiplied by 22x, the valuation convention for this coverage. The multiple is unchanged from initiation.
- Prior rating and target in this session: Buy, $84.04, on FY+1 EPS of $3.82 at 22x. Both were superseded after the company revised guidance. See the revision note below.

## Thesis

Northgate reported a strong Q2, then cut its full-year guide on export-control restrictions. The current thesis is therefore a Hold, not a growth Buy:

1. Reported demand in Q2 was strong. Revenue was $412M, up 19% year over year, with gross margin of 41.2%, per the company Q2 earnings release.
2. The forward picture is materially weaker than the Q2 picture. Full-year revenue growth is now guided to 11%, down from 18%, and full-year gross margin is now guided to approximately 38.5%, down from approximately 41%, per the company guidance revision press release.
3. The stated cause of the cut is regulatory, not a reported collapse in end demand. The company cited new export-control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries. That distinction is why this is a Hold rather than a Sell, and it is also why the rating cannot stay at Buy until the scope of the restrictions is known.
4. Valuation now reflects the cut in full. The FY+1 EPS estimate is $3.05, down from $3.82, a cut of about 20.2%, with the 22x multiple held constant so no multiple expansion is used to offset lower earnings.

## Revision note: what changed and why

This report was initiated in the same session on the Q2 materials, then revised when Northgate issued its guidance revision press release mid-session.

- Old guide, from the Q2 earnings release: 18% full-year revenue growth, gross margin of approximately 41%.
- New guide, from the guidance revision press release: 11% full-year revenue growth, gross margin of approximately 38.5%.
- EPS followed the guide: $3.82 became $3.05.
- Price target followed EPS at an unchanged multiple: $84.04 became $67.10.
- Rating followed the thesis: Buy became Hold.

No Q2 reported figure was restated in the revision release. The change is entirely in forward guidance and in the estimates built on it.

## Risks

### Export-control risk disclosure (required with any published price target)

New export-control restrictions are the stated reason for the guidance cut in the company guidance revision press release. The restrictions affect shipments to two countries. The release does not specify which product lines or SKUs are affected, so the full revenue and margin exposure cannot be quantified from the release alone. Further restrictions, an expansion to additional countries, products, or SKUs, or a longer duration than the current guide assumes could lead to further cuts to revenue growth, gross margin, and EPS. Conversely, a narrower scope than feared is the main route by which the revised guide could prove conservative. Any published price target for this name must be accompanied by this export-control risk disclosure section.

### Other risks

- Guidance risk: the revised guide of 11% growth and approximately 38.5% gross margin is a company forecast, not a reported result. One cut in this session establishes that the guide can move.
- Margin risk: the guided margin step down from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5% is about 2.5 percentage points. If the mix or volume effect of the restrictions is larger than the guide assumes, margin and EPS have further downside.
- Demand risk: Q2 data-center demand was described by management in the Q2 earnings release as strong, and our distributor check was steady for Q2. Neither source establishes demand after the revision, and a later demand softening would compound the regulatory cut.
- Competitive risk, stated with its limits: a competitor firm's note, which is not a citable source under firm policy and cited no primary data, claimed share loss in AI accelerator sockets. That claim is not used as a basis for any figure, rating, or factual claim in this report. It is noted here only as an unverified claim under monitoring. If primary evidence on share appears, the thesis should be revisited.

## What would change this rating

- Back to Buy: primary evidence that the export-control scope is narrow and contained, plus evidence that the revised guide of 11% growth and approximately 38.5% gross margin is holding or conservative.
- To Sell: a further guidance cut, primary evidence that end demand, not only export controls, is deteriorating, or primary evidence confirming material share loss.
