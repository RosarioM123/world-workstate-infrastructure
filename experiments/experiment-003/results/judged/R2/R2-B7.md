# Continuation Document: Onboarding User Study Plan

## 1. Where things stand

- Study: moderated user study of a new onboarding flow.
- Current format: remote moderated sessions.
- Session length used for planning: 25 minutes per session.
- Participant incentive: $30 per participant.

Decisions that currently govern the study:

- Plan each session at 25 minutes. Reason: a pilot run of the session script took 25 minutes, and a measured pilot is stronger evidence than the initial pre-pilot estimate.
- Pay each participant $30. Reason: an updated budget memo from the research lead set this figure in order to keep the 12 participant study within the total study budget cap of $900.
- Aim for 12 participants and continue recruitment until the remaining places are filled. Reason: only 3 participants have confirmed against the target of 12.

Decisions or instructions no longer in force:

- In-person moderated sessions in the usability lab. This was the format in the plan as first drafted. It is superseded by the remote format and remains in the record only as superseded.
- A session length of 45 minutes. This was the original planning figure. It is superseded by the 25 minute pilot-based figure.
- An incentive of $50 per participant. This came from the original project brief. It is superseded by the $30 figure in the later budget memo.
