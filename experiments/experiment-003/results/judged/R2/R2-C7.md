# Continuation Document: Onboarding User Study Plan (R2)

## 1. Where things stand

- Study format: Remote moderated sessions. This is the current format.
- Session length used for planning: 25 minutes per session.
- Participant incentive: $30 per participant.
- Target size: 12 participants.
- Recruitment status: Recruitment is falling short and is not complete. Only 3 of 12 participants have confirmed. Recruitment for the remaining places is still open.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions that currently govern the study:

- Use remote moderated sessions. Decision by the research lead. Reason: Recruitment evidence showed lab scheduling was the main friction cited by recruits, and remote sessions remove that friction. Remote sessions also fall within the existing IRB approval, which covers remote moderated sessions only, so no IRB amendment is needed for this format.
- Use 25 minutes per session for planning. Reason: A pilot run of the session script took 25 minutes, and a measured pilot run is stronger evidence than the initial pre-pilot estimate.
- Use an incentive of $30 per participant. Source: Updated budget memo from the research lead, who is the budget owner. Reason given in that memo: to keep the 12 participant study within the $900 budget cap.
- Keep the target at 12 participants. No change to the target size is recorded in the handoff material.

Decisions or instructions no longer in force:

- In-person moderated sessions in the usability lab. This was the original format and was reversed in favour of remote moderated sessions.
- A session length of 45 minutes per session. This was the initial planning estimate and was corrected to 25 minutes.
- An incentive of $50 per participant. This came from the original project brief, written last month, and was superseded by the $30 figure in the updated budget memo.

## 5. What changed and why

Change 1: Study format.

- From: In-person moderated sessions in the usability lab, as drafted at the start of the session in the original study plan (scenario Part 1).
- To: Remote moderated sessions.
- Why: Recruitment was falling short, with only 3 of 12 participants confirmed, and lab scheduling was the main friction cited by recruits. Remote sessions remove that friction and are covered by the existing IRB approval.
- Source: Recruitment update evidence in scenario Part 2, and the decision by the research lead recorded there.
- Authoritative now: Remote moderated sessions. The in-person version is superseded and is kept in the record only to show what changed and why.

Change 2: Session length.

- From: 45 minutes per session, the initial planning estimate in the original study plan (scenario Part 1), made before any pilot run.
- To: 25 minutes per session for planning.
- Why: A pilot run of the session script took 25 minutes, not 45 minutes. Measured pilot evidence is stronger than the pre-pilot estimate, so the original assumption was invalidated.
- Source: Pilot timing result evidence in scenario Part 2.
- Authoritative now: 25 minutes. The 45 minute figure is retained only as the superseded, corrected estimate.

Change 3: Participant incentive, including a conflicting instruction.

- From: $50 per participant, from the original project brief, written last month, recorded in scenario Part 1. This was the figure in force when drafting began.
- To: $30 per participant.
- Why: The updated budget memo stated the $30 figure was to keep the 12 participant study within the $900 cap.
- Source: Updated budget memo from the research lead, the budget owner, in scenario Part 2.
- Conflict and resolution: The $50 brief instruction and the $30 memo instruction conflict. The handoff material records the rule that, where two instructions conflict, the later one from the instruction owner governs. The memo is later, and its source is the research lead, who owns the budget.
- Authoritative now: $30 per participant. The $50 figure is superseded, with its provenance and the reason for supersession kept visible.

No other changes are recorded in the handoff material.
