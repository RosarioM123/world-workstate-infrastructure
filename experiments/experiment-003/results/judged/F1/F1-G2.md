# Continuation Document: Northgate Semiconductor (NGS)

## 1. Where things stand

Current rating: Hold.

Current price target: $67.10.

Current key figures, as they stand now:

- Q2 reported revenue: $412M, up 19% year over year.
- Q2 reported gross margin: 41.2%.
- Full-year revenue growth guide: 11%. This is the revised guide and is authoritative now.
- Full-year gross margin guide: approximately 38.5%. This is the revised guide and is authoritative now.
- FY+1 EPS estimate: $3.05.
- Valuation convention: 22x FY+1 EPS. Calculation: $3.05 multiplied by 22 equals $67.10.
- Basis for the forward figures: the Northgate guidance revision press release. Basis for the reported quarter: the Q2 earnings release.

The prior figures of 18% full-year revenue growth, approximately 41% full-year gross margin, $3.82 FY+1 EPS, and an $84.04 price target are superseded and are no longer current.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions that currently govern this coverage:

- Rating is Hold. Reason: the supports for the prior Buy rating, growth at 18%, margin near 41%, and EPS of $3.82, were all cut in one primary source update, the EPS estimate is down about 20.2%, and the scope of the export-control restrictions is unspecified, so it cannot yet be judged whether 11% growth and $3.05 EPS are a floor or a first cut.
- Price target is $67.10, based on FY+1 EPS of $3.05 at the unchanged 22x multiple. Reason: the multiple convention was not changed to soften the cut, so the full effect of the lower EPS flows into the target.
- The rating was not moved to Sell. Reason: the company did not attribute the cut to collapsing end demand, the Q2 reported figures were not restated, and there is no primary evidence in the prior session of the share loss claimed in the competitor note.
- The competitor note is not cited in any client-facing claim, and the thesis was not changed on the basis of it. Reason: it cited no primary data, its channel claim conflicted with the firm's own distributor check, and firm policy states that competitor notes are not a citable source for client-facing claims. Its share claim is treated as a flag to monitor, not as evidence.
- The distributor channel check is treated as supporting evidence, not as a primary source for client-facing factual claims. Reason: it is call notes from a channel contact, not a company release or filing.
- The downgrade rests on the company's own guidance revision press release, a primary source, not on the competitor note.
- Superseded Part 1 figures were not overwritten. The old guide, old EPS, and old price target are kept as superseded or corrected, with the reasons recorded.

Decisions that are no longer in force:

- Initial price target of $84.04, based on $3.82 FY+1 EPS at 22x. Superseded by $67.10. The journal records that it was correct on the old guide and is wrong on the new one.

- The guidance revision press release did not specify which product lines or SKUs are affected.

## 5. What changed and why

All changes below came from Part 2 of the prior session, after Northgate issued a guidance revision press release mid-session. That press release is a primary source, so it overrides the earlier guide from the Q2 release. The revised versions are authoritative now.

Figures and estimates:

- Full-year revenue growth guide: changed from 18% to 11%, a cut of 7 percentage points. Why: the guidance revision press release revised the guide, citing new export-control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries.
- Full-year gross margin guide: changed from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, a cut of about 2.5 percentage points. Why: the same guidance revision press release.
- FY+1 EPS estimate: changed from $3.82 to $3.05, a cut of $0.77, or about 20.2%. Why: the estimates were revised after the guidance update.
- Price target: changed from $84.04 to $67.10. Why: the revised EPS of $3.05 was applied at the unchanged 22x multiple. Calculation: $3.05 multiplied by 22 equals $67.10. The old target is superseded, not deleted.
- Valuation multiple: did not change. It remained 22x FY+1 EPS.

Decisions and interpretation:

- Rating: changed from Buy to Hold. Why: the growth, margin, and EPS supports for Buy were all cut in the guidance revision, and the scope of the restrictions is unspecified, so whether the revised figures are a floor or a first cut is not yet known.
- Status of export-control risk: changed from a disclosed risk category and a disclosure requirement attached to any price target in Part 1, to a realised driver of guidance in Part 2. Why: the company cited new export-control restrictions as the reason for the guidance cut.
- Status of the initial growth and margin assumptions: the 18% growth assumption and the approximately 41% margin assumption were corrected to 11% and approximately 38.5% respectively and are no longer usable.

What did not change:

- Q2 reported figures were not restated. Q2 revenue of $412M, Q2 growth of 19%, and Q2 gross margin of 41.2% remain the reported quarter. What changed is the forward guide, not the reported past.
