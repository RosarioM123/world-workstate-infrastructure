# Continuation Document: Northgate Semiconductor (NGS)

## 1. Where things stand

Current rating: Hold.
Current price target: $67.10.
Current price target basis: revised FY+1 EPS estimate of $3.05 multiplied by 22x. The 22x multiple is the valuation convention for this coverage.

Current key figures:
- Reported Q2 revenue: $412M, up 19% year over year.
- Reported Q2 gross margin: 41.2%.
- Current full year revenue growth guide: 11%.
- Current full year gross margin guide: approximately 38.5%.
- Current FY+1 EPS estimate: $3.05.

These revised forward looking figures supersede the earlier guide and estimates. No Q2 reported figure was restated.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions currently in force:

- Rating is Hold, not Buy and not Sell. Reason: the supports for the initial Buy were cut in a single primary source update, with full year growth cut from 18% to 11%, full year gross margin cut from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, and FY+1 EPS cut from $3.82 to $3.05, while the scope of the export control restrictions is still unknown, so it is not yet possible to judge whether the revised figures are a floor or only a first cut.
- Price target is $67.10, based on $3.05 multiplied by 22x. Reason: estimates followed the revised guide in full and the multiple was held unchanged, with no multiple expansion used to soften the effect.
- Valuation multiple remains 22x. Reason: it is the valuation convention for this coverage and was deliberately left unchanged through the revision.
- Competitor sell side note is not cited for any figure, rating, or factual claim, and did not drive any decision. Reason: firm policy states competitor notes are not a citable source for client facing claims, the note cited no primary data, and its channel claim conflicted with our firm distributor channel check.
- Distributor channel check is treated as supporting evidence only, not as a primary source for client facing factual claims. Reason: it is call notes from a channel contact rather than a company release or filing, and it covered Q2 only.
- Competitor note claim of share loss in AI accelerator sockets remains an unverified flag to monitor only. Reason: there is no primary evidence in the prior session confirming it. It should be tested only if primary evidence appears, at which point the thesis should be revisited.

Decisions no longer in force:

- Initial rating of Buy. Superseded by Hold after the guidance revision.
- Initial price target of $84.04, based on FY+1 EPS of $3.82 at 22x. Superseded by $67.10.
- Initial FY+1 EPS estimate of $3.82. Superseded by $3.05.
- Initial full year guides of 18% revenue growth and approximately 41% gross margin as the current guide. Superseded by 11% and approximately 38.5%. The earlier target and guide were correct on the guidance available at the time and are wrong on the guidance that replaced them, so they are superseded rather than erased.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts, from primary sources:

- Q2 earnings release reported revenue of $412M, up 19% year over year, and gross margin of 41.2%.
- At the start of the session, the company was guiding to full year revenue growth of 18% and full year gross margin of approximately 41%, with management citing strong data center demand as the driver in Q2 materials.
- Guidance revision press release now expects full year revenue growth of 11% and full year gross margin of approximately 38.5%.
- The company gave new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries as the reason for the revision in that release.
- No Q2 reported figure was restated in the revision.

Other recorded evidence, not established as a primary source fact for client facing purposes:

- Firm distributor channel check reported data center orders steady through Q2, no cancellations, stable lead times, and end demand described as strong and boring. Prior work treated this as supporting evidence only.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions:

- Assumption: The revised guide of 11% growth, approximately 38.5% gross margin, and $3.05 EPS could be a floor. Confidence: not established. Prior work explicitly stated it is not yet possible to judge whether these are a floor or only a first cut because the restriction scope is unknown.
- Assumption: The guidance cut was caused by export controls rather than deteriorating end demand. Confidence: limited. This is the company explanation in its own release. Prior work recorded no primary source independently confirming the scope of the restrictions, and noted the Q2 demand picture is not directly contradicted by the revision release.
- Assumption: Demand remains as strong after the revision as it was in Q2. Confidence: not established. Both management commentary on strong Q2 data center demand and the distributor check relate to Q2 only.
- Assumption: A narrower export control scope than feared could make the revised guide conservative. Confidence: conditional only. Prior work identified this as the main route by which the revised guide could prove conservative, not as an established outcome.
- Assumption: Holding the 22x multiple remains appropriate through the revision. Confidence: treated as a standing convention in prior work, deliberately unchanged, rather than rederived in the material provided.

## 5. What changed and why

Source of the changes: the Northgate guidance revision press release issued mid session. As a primary source, it overrides the earlier guide. The earlier Q2 earnings release remains the source for reported Q2 figures.

Changes:

- Rating: changed from Buy to Hold. Why: growth, margin, and EPS supports for the Buy were all cut in the revision, and restriction scope remains unknown. Not moved to Sell because the company did not attribute the cut to collapsing end demand, Q2 figures were not restated, and there is no primary evidence confirming the competitor note share loss claim. Authoritative now: Hold.
- Price target: changed from $84.04 to $67.10. Why: FY+1 EPS was cut and the multiple was held at 22x. Authoritative now: $67.10.
- FY+1 EPS estimate: changed from $3.82 to $3.05, a cut of $0.77 or about 20.2%. Why: estimates followed the revised guide in full. Authoritative now: $3.05.
- Valuation multiple: unchanged at 22x. No change was made to soften the target cut.
- Full year revenue growth guide: changed from 18% to 11%, down 7 percentage points. Why: company guidance revision citing new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries. Authoritative now: 11%.
- Full year gross margin guide: changed from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, down about 2.5 percentage points. Why: same guidance revision. Authoritative now: approximately 38.5%.
- Status of export control risk: changed from a disclosed risk category to the realised driver of the guidance cut. Why: the revision release attributed the cut to new restrictions. This change also made the export control disclosure requirement substantive, not only a matter of format.
- Reported Q2 figures: unchanged. Revenue $412M, growth 19%, and gross margin 41.2% remain the reported quarter. Everything that changed is forward looking.

## 6. Open questions and missing information

Missing information is flagged here and has not been filled in:

- Which product lines or SKUs are affected by the export control restrictions. The release does not specify this. Prior work called this the biggest open item in the coverage, and without it the full revenue and margin exposure cannot be quantified from the release alone.
- The scope of the restrictions in other respects. The material does not provide an independently confirmed scope from a primary source.
- Which specific countries are the two countries referred to in the revision. Their names are not given in the handoff material.
- Duration of the restrictions, and whether the current guide assumes a duration that will hold. Not specified in the handoff material.
- Whether 11% growth, approximately 38.5% gross margin, and $3.05 EPS are a floor or only a first cut.
- Whether the mix or volume effect of the restrictions is larger than the guide assumes, which would create further downside to margin and EPS.
- Demand after the revision. Both Q2 management commentary and the Q2 distributor check relate to Q2 only, so later demand is unestablished. A later softening would compound the regulatory cut.
- Whether further restrictions will expand to additional countries, products, or SKUs, or last longer than the current guide assumes.
- Whether the competitor note claim of share loss in AI accelerator sockets is true. It remains unverified, with no primary evidence in the prior session.
- The revised guide itself is a company forecast, not a reported result, and it was cut once in the session, so it can move again.

## 7. Next actions

In order, without redoing work already completed in the prior session:

1. Establish the product line and SKU scope of the export control restrictions from primary sources.
2. Quantify, once that scope is known, the revenue and margin exposure that could not be quantified from the revision release alone.
3. Test whether the revised guide of 11% growth and approximately 38.5% gross margin is holding or conservative, using primary evidence.
4. Monitor for primary evidence on demand after the revision, separate from Q2 evidence.
5. Monitor for primary evidence on the unverified share loss claim, and revisit the thesis only if such evidence appears.
6. Apply the return to Buy criteria only if primary evidence shows the export control scope is narrow and contained and the revised guide is holding or conservative.
7. Apply the move to Sell criteria if there is a further guidance cut, primary evidence that end demand and not only export controls is deteriorating, or primary evidence confirming material share loss.
8. Until one of those bodies of evidence appears, maintain Hold at $67.10, keep position sizing within the 3% limit, attach the export control disclosure to any published target, and ground all client facing factual claims only in the Q2 earnings release and the guidance revision press release.

## 8. Draft client note

We move Northgate Semiconductor to Hold with a price target of $67.10, based on revised FY+1 EPS of $3.05 at 22x.

In its Q2 release, the company reported revenue of $412M, up 19% year over year, and gross margin of 41.2%. In a subsequent guidance revision release, the company cut full year revenue growth guidance from 18% to 11% and full year gross margin guidance from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, citing new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries. We cut FY+1 EPS from $3.82 to $3.05 and held the multiple unchanged.

Our note rests on the revised guide as stated by the company. The scope of the restrictions by product line or SKU was not specified in the release, so whether the revised guide is a floor remains unknown.

Export control risk disclosure: Export controls are the realised driver of this guidance cut. Further restrictions, expansion to additional countries, products, or SKUs, or a longer duration than the current guide assumes could lead to further cuts to growth, margin, and EPS.
