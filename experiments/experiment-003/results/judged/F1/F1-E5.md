## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts, distinct from assumptions:

- Q2 revenue was $412M, up 19% year over year, and Q2 gross margin was 41.2%, per the company Q2 earnings release.
- The Q2 release guided full-year revenue growth of 18% and full-year gross margin of approximately 41% at that point, and management cited strong data-center demand as the driver.
- The distributor channel check reported data-center orders steady through the quarter, no cancellations, stable lead times, and end demand described as "strong and boring".
- Northgate later issued a guidance revision press release guiding full-year revenue growth to 11% and full-year gross margin to approximately 38.5%.
- The company gave new export-control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries as the reason for the revision.
- That press release did not specify which product lines or SKUs are affected.
- The Q2 reported figures were not restated in the revision.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions:

- Assumption: Full-year revenue growth of 11% is the current working guide. Confidence: uncertain in the prior work. It is the latest primary source guide, but the prior work stated it cannot yet be judged whether 11% is a floor or a first cut because the restriction scope is unspecified.
- Assumption: Full-year gross margin of approximately 38.5% is the current working guide. Confidence: uncertain for the same reason as above. The prior work noted the margin cut means the restriction is not costless to mix or volume, whatever the exact scope turns out to be.
- Assumption: FY+1 EPS of $3.05 is the current working estimate. Confidence: uncertain in the prior work, which stated it cannot yet be judged whether $3.05 EPS is a floor or a first cut.
- Assumption: The company's attribution of the cut to export controls, rather than to end demand, explains the revision. Confidence: limited in the prior work. The attribution is the company's explanation in its own release, and the prior work had no primary source in the session that independently confirms the scope of the restrictions.
- Assumption: Data-center demand remained as described by the Q2 release and the distributor check. Confidence: supported for the period covered by those sources, but limited going forward. The check covered the quarter and predates the revision in session order, so it does not settle what happens to orders under the new restrictions.
- Superseded assumption: Full-year revenue growth of 18%. No longer usable. It was corrected to 11%.
- Superseded assumption: Full-year gross margin of approximately 41%. No longer usable. It was corrected to approximately 38.5%.

No other confidence level is stated in the handoff material for these assumptions.

## 5. What changed and why

All changes below came from the Northgate guidance revision press release, a primary source that overrides the earlier guide from the Q2 earnings release. The revised version is authoritative now in each case.

- Full-year revenue growth guide: changed from 18% to 11%, a cut of 7 percentage points. Why: new export-control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries, per the company.
- Full-year gross margin guide: changed from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, a cut of about 2.5 percentage points. Why: the same guidance revision.
- FY+1 EPS estimate: changed from $3.82 to $3.05, a cut of $0.77, or about 20.2%. Why: the revised guidance. The estimate was revised after the press release was read.
- Price target: changed from $84.04 to $67.10. Why: $3.05 multiplied by the unchanged 22x multiple equals $67.10. The old target was correct on the old guide and is wrong on the new one. The current target, $67.10, is authoritative.
- Rating: changed from Buy to Hold. Why: growth, margin, and EPS supports for the Buy were all cut in one primary source update, and the scope of the restriction is unspecified.
- Status of export controls: changed from a disclosed risk category and disclosure requirement in Part 1 to a realised driver of guidance in Part 2.

What did not change:

- Q2 revenue of $412M, Q2 growth of 19%, and Q2 gross margin of 41.2% remain the reported quarter.
- Valuation multiple remained 22x FY+1 EPS.
- The decision not to cite the competitor note did not change.

## 6. Open questions and missing information

Missing or unknown information is flagged here and has not been filled in:

- Which product lines or SKUs are affected by the export-control restrictions. Not specified in the guidance revision press release.
- The identity of the two countries referred to in the release. The handoff material says shipments to two countries are affected but does not name them.
- The full scope of the export-control restrictions. No primary source in the handoff material independently confirms the scope.
- Whether the revised guide holds, and whether 11% growth and $3.05 EPS are a floor or a first cut.
- Whether the competitor note's claim that Northgate is losing share in AI accelerator sockets to a rival is correct. It remains unverified, cited no primary data, and is an open question to test against primary evidence if any appears.
- What happens to orders under the new restrictions. The distributor check predates the revision and does not settle this.
