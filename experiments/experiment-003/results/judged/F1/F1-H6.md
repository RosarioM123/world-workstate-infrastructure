# Continuation Document: Northgate Semiconductor (NGS)

## 1. Where things stand

- Current rating: Hold.
- Current price target: $67.10.
- Valuation basis: FY+1 EPS estimate of $3.05 multiplied by 22x FY+1 EPS. The calculation is $3.05 x 22 = $67.10. The 22x multiple is unchanged.
- Reported Q2 figures, from the Q2 earnings release: Q2 revenue of $412M, up 19% year over year, and Q2 gross margin of 41.2%. These reported figures were not restated.
- Current full year guidance, from the guidance revision press release: revenue growth of 11% and gross margin guided to approximately 38.5%.
- Current thesis as it stands now: the name remains under coverage at the revised target while the scope of the export control restrictions is unspecified and it cannot yet be judged whether 11% growth and $3.05 EPS are a floor or a first cut. The prior Buy thesis rested on 18% growth, margin near 41%, and EPS of $3.82, and those supports are no longer in place.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions that currently govern this coverage:

- Rating is Hold. Reason: the figures supporting a Buy were cut in one primary source update, the EPS estimate is down about 20.2%, and the scope of the restriction is unspecified, so whether the revised guide is a floor cannot yet be judged. The rating was not moved to Sell because the company did not attribute the cut to collapsing end demand, Q2 reported figures were not restated, and there is no primary evidence in the session of the share loss claimed by a competitor note.
- Price target is $67.10, based on FY+1 EPS of $3.05 at an unchanged multiple of 22x. Reason: the lower EPS from the revised guidance flows fully into the target. The multiple was deliberately not changed to soften the cut.
- FY+1 EPS estimate is $3.05. Reason: revised following the guidance revision press release.
- Do not cite the competitor note in any client facing claim, and do not change the thesis on the basis of it. Reason: it cited no primary data, its channel claim conflicted with the firm's own distributor check, and firm policy states competitor notes are not a citable source for client facing claims.
- Keep position sizing subject to the standing 3% single name limit. Reason: this was the standing portfolio constraint recorded before any recommendation, and the initial sizing decision was kept subject to it.

Decisions that are no longer in force:

- Initial rating of Buy. Superseded by Hold in Part 2.
- Initial price target of $84.04. Superseded by $67.10. It was based on the old guide and is described in the handoff as correct on the old guide and wrong on the new one.
- Initial FY+1 EPS estimate of $3.82. Superseded by $3.05.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts, distinct from assumptions:

- Q2 revenue was $412M, up 19% year over year, and Q2 gross margin was 41.2%, as reported in the Q2 earnings release, a primary source.
- The Q2 release at that time guided full year revenue growth to 18% and full year gross margin to approximately 41%, and cited strong data center demand as the driver. Those forward figures have since been superseded, as recorded in section 5.
- The guidance revision press release, a primary source, revised full year revenue growth to 11% and full year gross margin to approximately 38.5%, and gave the reason as new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries.
- The Q2 reported figures were not restated in the revision. The change was to the forward guide, not to the reported quarter.
- The distributor channel check, call notes from a channel contact at a major distributor, reported data center orders steady through the quarter, no cancellations, stable lead times, and end demand described by the contact as "strong and boring". The prior work treated this as supporting evidence, not as a primary source for client facing factual claims.

Assumptions the thesis rests on, labeled as assumptions:

- Assumption: The revised full year guide of 11% revenue growth is the current basis for coverage. Confidence: it comes from a primary source and is authoritative over the earlier guide, but the prior work explicitly stated it cannot yet be judged whether 11% growth is a floor or a first cut, because the scope of the restrictions is unspecified.
- Assumption: The revised full year gross margin guide of approximately 38.5% is the current basis for coverage. Confidence: same basis and same limitation as the revenue guide above. The prior work noted the cut means the restriction is not costless to mix or volume, whatever the exact scope turns out to be.
- Assumption: FY+1 EPS of $3.05, derived from the revised guidance at an unchanged 22x multiple, is the current estimate. Confidence: the prior work stated it cannot yet be judged whether $3.05 EPS is a floor or a first cut, for the same scope reason.
- Assumption: The company's attribution of the guidance cut to export control restrictions, rather than to a demand collapse, explains the change. Confidence: limited. This attribution is the company's explanation in its own release. The prior work stated there is no primary source in the session that independently confirms the scope of the restrictions.
- Assumption: The data center demand picture from Q2 and the distributor check is not directly contradicted by the revision. Confidence: limited to the period covered. The prior work noted the check covered the quarter and predates the revision, and does not settle what happens to orders under the new restrictions.
- Unverified claim, not treated as an assumption or fact: the competitor note claim that Northgate is losing share in AI accelerator sockets to a rival and that channel checks are turning. The prior work left this as an open question to test against primary evidence if any appears, and did not base the thesis or the downgrade on it.

## 5. What changed and why

All changes below came from the guidance revision press release issued mid session by Northgate, a primary source that overrides the earlier guide from the Q2 earnings release, unless otherwise stated.

- Full year revenue growth guide: changed from 18% to 11%, a cut of 7 percentage points. Why: the company cited new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries. Authoritative now: 11%.
- Full year gross margin guide: changed from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, a cut of about 2.5 percentage points. Why: same guidance revision press release and same stated reason. Authoritative now: approximately 38.5%.
- FY+1 EPS estimate: changed from $3.82 to $3.05, a cut of $0.77, or about 20.2%. Why: revised in response to the revised guidance. Source of the change: the prior analyst's revised working estimates in Step 8, following the press release. Authoritative now: $3.05.
- Price target: changed from $84.04 to $67.10. Why: $3.05 multiplied by the unchanged 22x multiple equals $67.10, whereas $3.82 multiplied by 22 equaled $84.04. The multiple did not change. Authoritative now: $67.10. The old target is superseded, not deleted.
- Rating: changed from Buy to Hold. Why: the growth, margin, and EPS supports for Buy were all cut, and the scope of the restriction is unspecified, so the durability of the revised figures cannot yet be judged. Authoritative now: Hold.
- Status of export control risk: changed from a disclosed risk category and a disclosure requirement attached to any price target, to a realised driver of guidance. Why: the revised guidance was attributed by the company to new export control restrictions.
- Status of the earlier growth and margin assumptions: the 18% growth assumption and the approximately 41% margin assumption are corrected and are no longer usable.

What did not change:

- Q2 reported revenue of $412M, Q2 growth of 19%, and Q2 gross margin of 41.2% were not restated.
- The valuation convention of 22x FY+1 EPS did not change.
- The decisions on the competitor note and the channel check did not change. Neither was used as the basis for the downgrade, which rests on the company's own guidance revision press release.
