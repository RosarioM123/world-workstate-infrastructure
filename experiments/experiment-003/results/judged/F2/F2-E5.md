# Continuation Document: Harborline Retail Credit Review

## 1. Where things stand

- Current internal leverage figure used for risk decisions: 4.2x, from the company 10-Q filing, including operating leases, on the same earnings basis as the earlier agency figure.
- Position decision: hold the existing Harborline 2029 bonds position, at 2.8% of portfolio, and do not add.
- Watchlist status: Harborline Retail is on the watchlist. While it is on the watchlist, no new purchases are permitted under the standing constraints.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions currently governing this position:

- Use 4.2x as the internal leverage figure. Reason: the 10-Q filing is the primary source and includes operating leases, giving a fuller total obligations basis.
- Hold the existing 2.8% position and make no new purchases. Reasons: corrected leverage is materially higher at 4.2x, the covenant claim is unresolved, and holiday-season sales for the next quarter have not yet been reported. No material requires a sale, and the position remains within the 5% single-issuer limit.
- Place Harborline Retail on the watchlist. Reasons: corrected leverage of 4.2x, the unconfirmed and unresolved covenant claim, and dependence on unreported holiday-season sales.
- Treat the reported possible covenant breach as unconfirmed and unresolved. Reason: it must not be treated as an established fact, and the company denial alone must not be treated as proof that no breach occurred.
- Limit the liquidity conclusion to the period stated in the filing: cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year. Reason: the handoff material does not support extending that conclusion beyond that period.

Decisions no longer in force:

- Provisional internal leverage view of 3.1x, from the rating agency report, excluding operating leases. Superseded by 4.2x.
- Provisional position stance to hold and consider adding on price dips, including the initial desk note description "leverage comfortable at 3.1x; consider adding on price dips". The case for adding is superseded and reversed.
- Part 1 position that no watchlist action was indicated. Superseded by the decision to place Harborline Retail on the watchlist.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts, as recorded in the handoff material:

- The portfolio holds Harborline 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio.
- At 2.8%, the position is within the 5% single-issuer limit, with headroom of 2.2 percentage points on the figures given.
- The rating agency report states leverage at 3.1x with a stable outlook and excludes operating lease liabilities. It is a secondary source.
- The company 10-Q filing shows total obligations including operating leases that imply leverage of 4.2x on the same earnings basis as the earlier figure. It is a primary source.
- The difference between the two leverage figures is 1.1x, explained in the material by the different obligations included, not by a change in earnings.
- A liquidity note taken from the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year.
- The 10-Q filing contains no mention of any covenant breach.
- No filing or lender statement confirming a covenant breach is recorded in the material.
- The company has denied the covenant claim in a short statement.

Assumptions:

- Assumption: the review can rely on the figures as provided in the agency report and the 10-Q filing for the bases stated. Confidence: no confidence level for this assumption is recorded in the handoff material.
- Assumption: the 4.2x and 3.1x figures are comparable because they use the same earnings basis, as stated in the material. Confidence: no separate confidence level is recorded in the handoff material.
- No other assumptions, and no confidence levels for assumptions, are explicitly recorded in the handoff material. Missing confidence information is flagged here rather than supplied by assertion.

Unconfirmed reports, kept distinct from facts and assumptions:

- A financial news article, citing unnamed sources, claims Harborline "may have breached a debt covenant". This is unconfirmed. It is not established as a fact, and the company denial alone is not treated as proof that no breach occurred.

## 4. Constraints

Standing portfolio constraints that bind action on this position:

- The portfolio must not use leverage: no margin borrowing, no leveraged funds or ETFs, and no derivatives that create leveraged exposure. This constraint governs the portfolio's own use of leverage. It is not a limit on an issuer's leverage ratio.
- Single-issuer limit: 5% of portfolio. The current 2.8% position is within this limit.
- Watchlist names: no new purchases while on the watchlist. Harborline Retail is now on the watchlist, so this constraint bars new purchases.
