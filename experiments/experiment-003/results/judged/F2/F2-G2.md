# Continuation Document: Harborline Retail Credit Review (F2)

## 1. Where things stand

- Current internal leverage figure used for risk decisions: 4.2x. This figure comes from the company 10-Q filing (E-4), includes operating leases, and is on the same earnings basis as the agency figure. It governs under decision D-2.
- Position decision: Hold the Harborline Retail 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio and make no new purchases (D-3). The earlier stance of considering adding on price dips does not stand.
- Watchlist status: Harborline Retail is on the watchlist as at the end of the prior session (D-4).

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions currently in force:

- D-2: Filing governs, internal leverage figure corrected to 4.2x.
  Reason: The filing is the primary source and uses the fuller obligations basis, including operating leases. The agency report is a secondary source and its figure explicitly excludes operating lease liabilities, so it understates total obligations. The 1.1x difference is explained by the obligations included, on the same earnings basis.

- D-3: Hold the existing position, do not add.
  Reason: Corrected leverage at 4.2x removes the basis for the add stance. The covenant claim is unresolved, and holiday-season sales for the next quarter are unreported, so no confirmed improvement supports adding. No material requires a sale: the position is within the 5% limit and the filing states maturity coverage through Q1 of next year. Watchlist placement under D-4 also bars new purchases by rule.

- D-4: Place Harborline Retail on the watchlist.
  Reason: Three factors together support placement: corrected leverage of 4.2x on a total obligations basis, an unconfirmed and unresolved covenant breach claim that needs a confirmed source to settle, and dependence on holiday-season sales that have not yet been reported.

- D-5: No finding that a covenant breach occurred.
  Reason: The only support in the materials is a news article citing unnamed sources, using the wording "may have breached". The company has denied the claim. No filing or lender statement confirms a breach, and the 10-Q filing contains no mention of any covenant breach. The denial alone is also not treated as proof that no breach occurred.

Decisions no longer in force:

- D-1: Treat leverage as comfortable at 3.1x and consider adding on price dips.
  Status: Reversed, superseded by D-2 and D-3. It was the initial desk note, based on the rating agency report in Part 1, which stated leverage of 3.1x with a stable outlook, before any primary filing was available. Its leverage premise was corrected to 4.2x.

The Part 1 position of no watchlist action was also superseded by D-4. The handoff records that this was never recorded as a formal decision.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts, distinct from assumptions:

- The portfolio holds Harborline Retail 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio (E-3).
- The rating agency report stated leverage of 3.1x with a stable outlook, and that figure excludes operating lease liabilities (E-1). This is established as what the agency reported on that basis, not as the governing total leverage figure.
- The company 10-Q filing states that total obligations including operating leases imply leverage of 4.2x on the same earnings basis as the agency figure, and the filing contains no mention of any covenant breach (E-4).
- The liquidity note taken from the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year, and that holiday-season sales are the key swing factor for the next quarter and have not been reported yet (E-7). No cash amount, revolver amount, or sales figure is given in the materials.
- The company has denied the covenant breach claim in a short statement (E-6). This is established as a denial having been made, not as proof that no breach occurred.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions, with confidence:

- A-2 (active, high confidence): The filing and agency figures share the same earnings basis, so the difference reflects obligations included, not earnings. Validated by E-4, which states the figure is on the same earnings basis.

- A-3 (validated, medium confidence): Cash and revolver availability cover Harborline maturities through Q1 of next year. From the liquidity note in the filing (E-7), a primary source. Confidence is medium rather than high because the materials give the coverage statement without underlying cash or revolver amounts. This assumption is not extended beyond Q1 of next year.

- A-4 (active, medium confidence): At the end of the prior session, no covenant breach by Harborline Retail is established by a confirmed source. This is an assumption about the state of the evidence, not a finding about whether a breach in fact occurred. It is supported negatively by the absence of any mention of a breach in the filing (E-4) and by the absence of any confirming filing or lender statement in the materials.

- A-5 (active, medium confidence): Holiday-season sales are the key swing factor for the next quarter, as stated in E-7. The sales outcome itself is unvalidated because the sales have not been reported.

Invalidated assumption:

- A-1 (invalidated, low confidence): The agency leverage figure of 3.1x represents Harborline leverage for internal purposes. Invalidated by E-4, because E-1 excludes operating lease liabilities and E-4, on the same earnings basis including those liabilities, implies 4.2x.

Unconfirmed report, not a fact and not an assumption:

- E-5: A news article claims Harborline "may have breached a debt covenant", citing unnamed sources. It is recorded as unconfirmed. No filing or lender statement confirms the claim, and it is not evidence that a breach occurred.

## 4. Constraints

Every standing portfolio constraint in the handoff that binds action on this position:

- C-1: The portfolio must not use leverage: no margin borrowing, no leveraged funds or ETFs, no derivatives that create leveraged exposure. This governs the portfolio's own leverage use. It is not an issuer leverage limit.
- C-2: Single-issuer limit: 5% of portfolio. The current holding at 2.8% is within this limit.
- C-3: Watchlist names: no new purchases while on the watchlist. Harborline Retail is on the watchlist, so this bars new purchases.

The handoff also records a task rule, C-4: use only the scenario materials, distinguish what each source says from what is established, record provenance for every figure, keep an earlier figure visible as corrected when a figure is corrected, and treat an unconfirmed report as not a fact.
