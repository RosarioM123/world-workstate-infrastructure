Northgate Semiconductor (NGS): Downgrade to Hold, price target $67.10

We move NGS to Hold with a price target of $67.10, based on FY+1 EPS of $3.05 at 22x.

What changed and why: In its guidance revision press release, the company cut full-year revenue growth guidance from 18% to 11% and gross margin guidance from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, citing new export-control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries. Our FY+1 EPS estimate falls from $3.82 to $3.05. Reported Q2 figures were revenue of $412M, up 19% year over year, and gross margin of 41.2%, per the company Q2 earnings release. Until that scope is disclosed, we cannot judge whether the revised guidance is a floor. Further restrictions or a wider scope could lead to further cuts to guidance, estimates, and the target.

Sources: Company Q2 earnings release and company guidance revision press release, both primary sources.
