# Continuation Document: Northgate Semiconductor (NGS)

## 1. Where things stand

Current rating: Hold.

Current price target: $67.10. This is based on a revised FY+1 EPS estimate of $3.05 multiplied by 22x. The 22x multiple is the valuation convention for this coverage and was left unchanged through the revision.

Current key figures for the thesis, as they stand now:

- Full year revenue growth guidance: 11%.
- Full year gross margin guidance: approximately 38.5%.
- FY+1 EPS estimate: $3.05.
- Q2 reported results (not restated): revenue of $412M, up 19% year over year, and gross margin of 41.2%.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions that currently govern this coverage:

- Rating is Hold. Reason: the supports for the prior Buy were cut in a single primary source update, namely growth, margin, and EPS, and the scope of the export control restrictions is still unknown, so it cannot yet be judged whether the revised guide and EPS are a floor or only a first cut.
- Price target is $67.10 at 22x on FY+1 EPS of $3.05. Reason: estimates followed the revised guidance in full, with no multiple expansion used to soften the effect.
- Valuation multiple is held at 22x. Reason: this is the valuation convention for this coverage and was deliberately left unchanged.
- Rating is not moved to Sell. Reason: the company did not attribute the cut to collapsing end demand, Q2 figures were not restated, and there is no primary evidence in the prior session confirming the share loss claimed by a competitor note.
- A competitor sell side note is not cited as a source for any figure, rating, or factual claim. Reason: firm policy states competitor notes are not a citable source for client facing claims, the note cited no primary data, and its channel claim conflicted with the firm's own distributor check.
- The distributor channel check is treated as supporting evidence only, not as a primary source for client facing factual claims. Reason: it is call notes from a channel contact rather than a company release or filing, and it covered Q2 only.

Decisions that are no longer in force:

- Initial Buy rating. Superseded by Hold.
- Initial price target of $84.04, based on FY+1 EPS of $3.82 at 22x. Superseded by $67.10.
- Use of the original full year guidance of 18% revenue growth and approximately 41% gross margin as the current forward guide. Superseded by the revised guidance.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts, from primary sources:

- Q2 earnings release reported revenue of $412M, up 19% year over year, and gross margin of 41.2%.
- The company originally guided to full year revenue growth of 18% and full year gross margin of approximately 41%, with management citing strong data center demand as the driver.
- A later guidance revision press release guided full year revenue growth to 11% and full year gross margin to approximately 38.5%.
- The company gave new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries as the reason for the revision.
- No Q2 reported figure was restated in the revision.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions:

- Assumption: the original 18% full year guide looked supported rather than aspirational, based on reported growth near 19%, margin near 41%, and channel evidence of steady demand. Confidence in the prior work: supported by the Q2 release and the distributor check, but not independently proven for client facing purposes. This assumption related to the original guide, which has been superseded.
- Assumption: FY+1 EPS of $3.05 and the revised guide of 11% growth and approximately 38.5% gross margin will hold. Confidence: low certainty stated in the prior work. The guide is a company forecast, not a reported result, it was cut once in the session, and whether it is a floor or a first cut is unknown because the restriction scope is unknown.
- Assumption: the guidance cut was caused by export controls rather than by deteriorating end demand. Confidence: this is the company explanation in its own release. The prior work recorded no primary source that independently confirmed the scope of the restrictions, and the Q2 demand picture is not directly contradicted by the revision release.
- Assumption: demand remains as described for Q2. Confidence: limited to Q2. Management commentary on strong data center demand and the distributor check reporting steady orders, no cancellations, stable lead times, and strong end demand both relate to Q2. Demand after the revision is unestablished.
- Unverified flag, not an assumption of the thesis: a competitor note claimed Northgate is losing share in AI accelerator sockets to a rival and that channel checks are turning. Confidence: unverified, with no primary data cited and a conflict with the firm's distributor check. It did not drive any decision.

## 5. What changed and why

Everything that changed during the prior session:

- Full year revenue growth guidance changed from 18% to 11%, a change of 7 percentage points down. Why: the company issued a guidance revision press release citing new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries. Source: the guidance revision press release, a primary source that overrides the earlier guide. Authoritative now: 11%.
- Full year gross margin guidance changed from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, a change of about 2.5 percentage points down. Why: the same guidance revision and stated reason as above. Source: the guidance revision press release. Authoritative now: approximately 38.5%.
- FY+1 EPS estimate changed from $3.82 to $3.05, a cut of $0.77, or about 20.2%. Why: estimates followed the revised guidance in full. Source: the prior session's estimate revision following the guidance revision press release. Authoritative now: $3.05.
- Price target changed from $84.04 to $67.10. Why: the EPS cut at an unchanged 22x multiple ($3.82 by 22x was $84.04; $3.05 by 22x is $67.10). Source: the prior session's target revision. Authoritative now: $67.10.
- Rating changed from Buy to Hold. Why: growth at 18%, margin near 41%, and EPS of $3.82, which had supported the Buy, were all cut, and the scope of the restrictions remains unknown. Source: the prior session's rating decision following the guidance revision. Authoritative now: Hold.
- Status of export control risk changed from a disclosed risk category to the realised driver of the guidance cut. Why: the revision release attributed the cut to new export control restrictions. Source: the guidance revision press release.

What did not change: the 22x valuation multiple, and Q2 reported revenue of $412M, growth of 19%, and gross margin of 41.2%. The earlier target and guide were superseded rather than erased, because they were correct on the guidance available at the time.

## 6. Open questions and missing information

What is still unknown or undisclosed. Missing information is flagged and not filled in:

- Which product lines or SKUs are affected by the export control restrictions is not specified in the revision release. This is the biggest open item in the coverage.
- The full scope of the restrictions is unknown, so the full revenue and margin exposure cannot be quantified from the release alone.
- Which two countries are affected is not named in the handoff material.
- The duration of the restrictions and whether they will expand to additional countries, products, or SKUs is unknown.
- Whether 11% growth, approximately 38.5% gross margin, and $3.05 EPS are a floor or only a first cut is unknown.
- No primary source in the prior session independently confirmed the scope of the restrictions.
- Demand after the revision is unestablished. Available demand evidence relates to Q2 only.
- Whether the competitor note's claim of share loss in AI accelerator sockets is true is unverified. No primary evidence confirming it was present in the prior session.
- If the mix or volume effect of the restrictions is larger than the revised guide assumes, whether and by how much margin and EPS would fall further is unknown.

6. ## 8. Draft client note

Rating: Hold. Price target: $67.10, based on FY+1 EPS of $3.05 at 22x.

We are moving Northgate Semiconductor from Buy to Hold and cutting our price target from $84.04. In its guidance revision press release, the company cut full year revenue growth guidance from 18% to 11% and full year gross margin guidance from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, citing new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries. We cut FY+1 EPS from $3.82 to $3.05 and held the multiple at 22x. Q2 reported results were not restated: revenue was $412M, up 19% year over year, with gross margin of 41.2%, per the Q2 earnings release.

Our revised estimates assume the new guidance holds. That assumption has limited certainty, because the release does not specify which product lines or SKUs are affected, so the full exposure cannot yet be quantified.

Main risk and required disclosure: export control risk is the realised driver of this cut. Further restrictions, expansion to additional countries, products, or SKUs, or a longer duration than the current guide assumes could lead to further cuts to growth, margin, and EPS. A narrower scope than feared is the main route by which the revised guide could prove conservative. Any published price target must be accompanied by the export control risk disclosure section.
