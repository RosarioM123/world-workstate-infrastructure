# Continuation Document: Northgate Semiconductor (NGS)

## 1. Where things stand

Current rating: Hold
Current price target: $67.10

The current price target is based on a revised FY+1 EPS estimate of $3.05 multiplied by 22x. The 22x multiple is the valuation convention for this coverage.

Current key figures for the thesis, as they stand now:

- Reported Q2 revenue: $412M, up 19% year over year
- Reported Q2 gross margin: 41.2%
- Current full year revenue growth guidance: 11%
- Current full year gross margin guidance: approximately 38.5%
- Current FY+1 EPS estimate: $3.05
- Valuation multiple: 22x

The Q2 figures are reported results from the company Q2 earnings release. The full year growth and margin figures are the revised company guidance from the guidance revision press release, not reported results.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions that currently govern this coverage:

- **Rating is Hold.** Reason: the supports for the prior Buy thesis, full year growth of 18%, gross margin near 41%, and FY+1 EPS of $3.82, were all cut in a single primary source update. The scope of the export control restrictions is still unknown, so it is not yet possible to judge whether 11% growth and $3.05 in EPS are a floor or only a first cut.
- **Price target is $67.10, based on FY+1 EPS of $3.05 at 22x.** Reason: estimates followed the revised guidance in full, and the multiple was held unchanged so no multiple expansion was used to soften the effect of the EPS cut.
- **Valuation multiple remains 22x.** Reason: this is the valuation convention for this coverage and was deliberately left unchanged through the revision.
- **Coverage remains in place at Hold until specified evidence appears.** Reason: a return to Buy or a move to Sell each requires primary evidence that is not yet available, as detailed in section 7.
- **The rating was not moved to Sell.** Reason: the company did not attribute the cut to collapsing end demand, the Q2 figures were not restated, and there is no primary evidence in the prior session confirming the share loss claimed by the competitor note.
- **Competitor sell side note is not used as a source.** Reason: it cited no primary data, its channel claim conflicted with our firm distributor channel check, and firm policy states competitor notes are not a citable source for client facing claims. It did not drive any decision in the prior session. Its share loss claim is retained only as an unverified flag to monitor.
- **Distributor channel check is treated as supporting evidence only, not as a primary source for client facing factual claims.** Reason: it is call notes from a channel contact rather than a company release or filing, and it covered Q2 only.

Decisions no longer in force:

- **Initial rating of Buy.** Superseded by the Hold rating after the guidance revision.
- **Initial price target of $84.04.** Superseded by the $67.10 target. It was based on FY+1 EPS of $3.82 at 22x and was correct on the guidance available at the time.
- **Initial FY+1 EPS estimate of $3.82.** Superseded by the $3.05 estimate.
- **Initial full year guidance of 18% revenue growth and approximately 41% gross margin as the governing guide.** Superseded by the revised guidance. These remain the prior guide figures for comparison, not the current guide.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts, from primary sources:

- Q2 revenue was $412M, up 19% year over year, and Q2 gross margin was 41.2%, as reported in the company Q2 earnings release. No Q2 reported figure was restated in the guidance revision.
- The company initially guided to full year revenue growth of 18% and full year gross margin of approximately 41%, citing strong data center demand.
- The company later issued a guidance revision press release guiding full year revenue growth to 11% and full year gross margin to approximately 38.5%.
- The company gave new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries as the reason for the revision in that release.

Supporting evidence, not an established primary source fact for client facing purposes:

- Our firm distributor channel check reported data center orders steady through Q2, no cancellations, and stable lead times, and described end demand as strong and boring. The prior work treated this as supporting evidence. It covered Q2, so it does not establish demand or orders after the revision.

Assumptions the thesis rests on:

- **Assumption: The revised guidance of 11% growth and approximately 38.5% gross margin is the current basis for estimates.** Confidence: this is the latest primary source guidance and is authoritative for estimates now, but the prior work stressed it is a company forecast, not a reported result, and that it was cut once in the session and can move again. Confidence that it is a floor is not established.
- **Assumption: FY+1 EPS of $3.05 follows from the revised guidance.** Confidence: it is the estimate adopted in full after the guide cut, at the unchanged 22x multiple. Its reliability depends on the revised guide holding and on the unknown scope of the restrictions.
- **Assumption: The guidance cut was caused by export controls rather than collapsing end demand.** Confidence: this is the company attribution in its own release. The demand picture from Q2 and the distributor check is not directly contradicted by the revision release, but no primary source in the session independently confirmed the scope of the restrictions.
- **Assumption: Demand after the revision has not been established to have deteriorated.** Confidence: low basis for any forward claim. Both management commentary on strong Q2 data center demand and the distributor check relate to Q2 only.
- **Assumption: A narrower export control scope than feared could make the revised guide prove conservative.** Confidence: identified in the prior work as the main route by which the revised guide could prove conservative, but scope itself is unknown, so this is unproven.

The competitor note claim that Northgate is losing share in AI accelerator sockets is not listed as a fact or as a thesis assumption. It is an unverified claim with no primary data cited.

## 5. What changed and why

Source of the changes: the company guidance revision press release issued mid session. As a primary source, it overrides the earlier guide from the Q2 earnings release. No change came from the competitor note or from the distributor channel check.

Changes during the prior session:

- **Full year revenue growth guidance: from 18% to 11%, down 7 percentage points.** Why: new export control restrictions affecting shipments to two countries, as stated by the company. Authoritative version now: 11%.
- **Full year gross margin guidance: from approximately 41% to approximately 38.5%, down about 2.5 percentage points.** Why: the same guidance revision. The margin cut shows the restriction is not costless to mix or volume. Authoritative version now: approximately 38.5%.
- **FY+1 EPS estimate: from $3.82 to $3.05, a cut of $0.77 or about 20.2%.** Why: estimates followed the revised guide in full. Authoritative version now: $3.05.
- **Price target: from $84.04 to $67.10.** Why: the EPS cut at the unchanged 22x multiple. No multiple expansion was used. Authoritative version now: $67.10.
- **Rating: from Buy to Hold.** Why: growth, margin, and EPS supports for the Buy were all cut, and the scope of the restrictions is unknown, so it cannot yet be judged whether the revised figures are a floor. Authoritative version now: Hold.
- **Status of export control risk: from a disclosed risk category to the realised driver of the guidance cut.** Why: the revision release attributed the cut to new restrictions. Authoritative treatment now: export control risk is a realised driver, and any published target requires the export control risk disclosure section.
- **Valuation multiple: no change.** It remained 22x throughout.

What did not change:

- Q2 reported revenue of $412M, Q2 growth of 19%, and Q2 gross margin of 41.2% were not restated.
- The standing constraints in section 4 remained in force.

The earlier target and guide were superseded rather than erased, because they were correct on the guidance available at the time and are wrong on the guidance that replaced it.

- Whether the competitor note claim of share loss in AI accelerator sockets is true. It remains unverified, with no primary evidence in the session confirming it.
- The names of the two countries affected by the restrictions. Not specified in the handoff material beyond the fact that shipments to two countries are affected.
- Current share price. Not provided in the handoff material, so no upside or downside to the target is stated here.
