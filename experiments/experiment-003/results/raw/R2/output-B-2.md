The study is a moderated user study of a new onboarding flow.

## 2. Decisions in force

1. Run the study as remote moderated sessions. Reason: recruitment was falling short, with only 3 of 12 participants confirmed, and lab scheduling was the main friction cited by recruits. Remote sessions remove the lab scheduling friction. This format also sits within the existing IRB approval.
2. Plan sessions at 25 minutes per session. Reason: a pilot run of the session script took 25 minutes, and a measured pilot is stronger evidence than the initial pre-pilot estimate.
3. Pay an incentive of $30 per participant. Reason: an updated budget memo from the research lead set this figure in order to keep the 12 participant study within the total study budget cap of $900.

1. In-person moderated sessions in the usability lab. This was the format in the plan as first drafted. It is superseded and remains in the record as superseded rather than deleted.
2. A session length of 45 minutes per session. This was the original planning estimate. It survives only as the corrected initial estimate.
3. An incentive of $50 per participant. This came from the original project brief. It is superseded and is kept visible only with its provenance and the reason it was replaced.
