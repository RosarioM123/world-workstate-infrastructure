  - Reason: A pilot run of the session script took 25 minutes. A measured pilot run is stronger evidence than the initial pre-pilot estimate.
- Participant incentive: $30 per participant.
  - Reason: The updated budget memo from the research lead, the budget owner, set this figure to keep the 12 participant study within the $900 budget cap.
- Target size: 12 participants.
  - Reason: This is the target size recorded in the plan and in the recruitment status. No change to the target size is recorded in the handoff material.

- In-person moderated sessions in the usability lab: Reversed and superseded by remote moderated sessions.
- Session length of 45 minutes per session: Superseded and corrected to 25 minutes. It is retained only as the superseded initial estimate.
- Participant incentive of $50 per participant: Superseded by $30 per participant. The $50 figure came from the original project brief, written last month.

## 5. What changed and why

Change 1: Study format.

- From: In-person moderated sessions in the usability lab.
- To: Remote moderated sessions.
- Why: Recruitment was falling short, with only 3 of 12 participants confirmed, and lab scheduling was the main friction cited by recruits. Remote sessions remove that friction and fall within the existing IRB approval, which covers remote moderated sessions only, so no IRB amendment is needed.
- Authoritative now: Remote moderated sessions.
- Source: Recruitment update evidence in scenario Part 2, and the decision by the research lead recorded there. The original in-person plan came from scenario Part 1.

Change 2: Session length.

- From: 45 minutes per session.
- To: 25 minutes per session.
- Why: A pilot run of the session script took 25 minutes, not 45 minutes. A measured pilot run is stronger evidence than the initial pre-pilot estimate.
- Authoritative now: 25 minutes per session for planning.
- Source: Pilot timing result evidence in scenario Part 2. The 45 minute figure came from the plan as drafted at session start in scenario Part 1.

Change 3: Participant incentive, including a conflicting instruction.

- From: $50 per participant.
- To: $30 per participant.
- Why: The updated budget memo set $30 per participant to keep the 12 participant study within the $900 cap.
- Authoritative now: $30 per participant.
- Source: The updated budget memo from the research lead, the budget owner, in scenario Part 2. The $50 figure came from the original project brief, written last month, described in scenario Part 1.
- Conflict and resolution: The $50 instruction in the original project brief conflicted with the later $30 instruction. Where two instructions conflict, the later one from the instruction owner governs. The memo is later and its source is the research lead, who owns the budget, so $30 governs and $50 no longer governs.

Note on the format change and an earlier position: The original in-person format also conflicted with the scope of the existing IRB approval, which covers remote sessions only. The remote format that now governs resolves that gap without an amendment.
