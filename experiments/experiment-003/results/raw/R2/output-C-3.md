# Continuation Document: Onboarding User Study Plan

## 1. Where things stand

- Study: A moderated user study of a new onboarding flow.
- Current format: Remote moderated sessions.
- Session length used for planning: 25 minutes per session.
- Participant incentive: $30 per participant.
- Target size: 12 participants.
- Recruitment status: Open and incomplete. Only 3 of 12 participants have confirmed.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions currently governing the study:

- Use remote moderated sessions. Reason: Recruitment was falling short and lab scheduling was the main friction cited by recruits. Remote sessions remove that friction and fall within the existing IRB approval, so no IRB amendment is needed for this format.
- Plan on 25 minutes per session. Reason: A pilot run of the session script took 25 minutes, and a measured pilot run is stronger evidence than the initial pre-pilot estimate.
- Pay an incentive of $30 per participant. Reason: The updated budget memo from the research lead, the budget owner, set this figure to keep the 12 participant study within the $900 cap.
- Keep the target at 12 participants. No change to the target was recorded in the handoff material.

Decisions or instructions no longer in force:

- In-person moderated sessions in the usability lab. Superseded by the remote format.
- A session length of 45 minutes per session. Superseded by the corrected 25 minute planning value.
- An incentive of $50 per participant. Superseded by the $30 figure from the updated budget memo.
