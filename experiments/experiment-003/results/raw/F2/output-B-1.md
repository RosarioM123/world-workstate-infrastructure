# Continuation Document: Harborline Retail Credit Review

## 1. Where things stand

- Current internal leverage figure used for risk decisions: 4.2x, from the company 10-Q filing. The figure reflects total obligations including operating leases.
- Position decision: hold the existing position and do not add. The holding is 2.8% of the portfolio. Nothing in the handoff material requires a sale.
- Watchlist status: Harborline Retail was placed on the watchlist during this review.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions currently in force:

- Use 4.2x from the 10-Q filing as the internal leverage figure for risk decisions. Reason: the filing is the primary source and captures total obligations including operating leases, on the same earnings basis as the earlier figure it replaced.
- Hold the existing position and make no new purchases. Reason: at 4.2x on a total obligations basis, the earlier description of leverage as comfortable no longer holds as the internal view, and the case for adding on price dips is not supported by the material. The watchlist rule also bars new purchases.
- Place Harborline Retail on the watchlist. Reason: the corrected leverage of 4.2x, an unresolved claim about a possible covenant breach, and dependence on holiday season sales that have not yet been reported.
- Treat the covenant breach claim as unresolved and unconfirmed, not as an established fact. Reason: no filing or lender statement confirms a breach, and the position decision was not based on treating the claim as if it had happened. The company's denial alone was also not treated as proof that no breach occurred. The claim, together with the higher corrected leverage, is a reason for closer monitoring rather than a finding.
- Limit the liquidity conclusion to the period covered by the filing. Reason: a liquidity note taken from the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year, and that conclusion was deliberately not extended beyond the period the filing covers.
- Use the filing basis for any monitoring. Reason: returning to the agency basis would repeat the understatement that shaped the start of this review.

Decisions no longer in force:

- The initial desk note stance that leverage was comfortable at 3.1x and that adding on price dips should be considered. It rested entirely on the rating agency report, was recorded at the time as provisional, and was superseded once the filing arrived.
- Use of 3.1x as the governing internal leverage figure. The 3.1x figure remains accurate as a statement of what the agency reported on its stated basis, but it is corrected for internal use and no longer governs.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts:

- The company 10-Q filing gives leverage of 4.2x on a total obligations basis including operating leases, on the same earnings basis as the earlier figure.
- The earlier figure was 3.1x from a rating agency report, a secondary source, with a stable outlook. That agency figure explicitly excluded operating lease liabilities.
- The difference between the two figures is 1.1x, and the material explains it by the obligations included rather than by any change in earnings.
- The holding is 2.8% of the portfolio, within the standing single issuer limit of 5%, leaving headroom of 2.2 percentage points on the figures given.
- A liquidity note taken from the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year, and no other material contradicted that statement.
- The filing identifies holiday season sales as the key swing factor for the next quarter.
- The 10-Q filing contains no mention of a covenant breach.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions:

- Assumption: where the agency report and the filing conflict, the filing governs. Basis given in the material: the filing is the primary source and captures operating leases. Confidence: high for internal use, as this is the stated basis of the corrected review.
- Assumption: the filing based liquidity statement can be relied on only for the period the filing covers, through Q1 of next year. Confidence: supported by the filing and uncontradicted by other material for that period. No confidence is stated for any later period.
- Assumption: no sales figure should be used for the period after the filing coverage until holiday season sales are reported. Confidence: the direction of the next quarter is unknown because those sales have not been reported.

The handoff material does not provide numeric confidence levels for these items. Confidence is therefore described qualitatively above, based only on the sources named in the material.

Unconfirmed report, kept distinct from facts and assumptions:

- A financial news article claimed that Harborline may have breached a debt covenant, citing unnamed sources. The company denied the claim in a short statement. The claim is unresolved and unconfirmed, as stated in Section 6.

## 4. Constraints

- Single issuer limit: 5% of the portfolio. The current holding of 2.8% complies, with headroom of 2.2 percentage points on the figures given.
- Watchlist rule: no new purchases are allowed while a name is on the watchlist. This rule now applies to Harborline Retail from its placement in this review.
- Portfolio leverage constraint: the portfolio must not itself use leverage, meaning no margin borrowing, no leveraged funds or exchange traded funds, and no derivatives that create leveraged exposure. This constraint governs the portfolio's own use of leverage and was not treated as a limit on an issuer's leverage ratio.

## 5. What changed and why

- Internal leverage figure: changed from 3.1x to 4.2x. Why: a second set of materials arrived containing the company 10-Q filing. The agency figure excluded operating lease liabilities, while the filing figure includes them as part of total obligations. The 1.1x difference is explained by the obligations included, not by any change in earnings. Authoritative now: 4.2x from the filing. Source of the change: the 10-Q filing, the primary source, which governs over the rating agency report, the secondary source.
- Internal description of leverage: changed from comfortable at 3.1x to a view under which comfortable no longer holds. Why: the corrected figure is 4.2x on a total obligations basis. Authoritative now: the corrected internal view based on the filing.
- Position stance: changed from the provisional initial desk note suggestion of considering adding on price dips to hold the existing position and do not add. Why: the stance depended on a secondary source whose figure omitted operating leases and was superseded once the filing arrived, and the watchlist rule now bars new purchases. Authoritative now: hold with no new purchases.
- Watchlist status: changed from not indicated by the first materials alone to placed on the watchlist. Why: the corrected leverage of 4.2x, the unresolved covenant breach claim, and dependence on unreported holiday season sales. Authoritative now: on the watchlist.

## 6. Open questions and missing information

- Whether any covenant breach occurred is still unknown and unconfirmed. Confirmation or refutation from a filing or a lender statement is missing. Neither is present in the material.
- Holiday season sales for the next quarter are still unreported, so the direction of the next quarter is still unknown.
- Leverage and liquidity beyond the periods and basis given in the filing materials provided are unknown.
- Any updated filing figure for leverage or liquidity is missing from the material.
- No sales figure is available in the material to fill the gap after the period covered by the filing, and none should be asserted.

## 7. Next actions

1. Look for a filing or lender statement that settles the covenant question.
2. Wait for the reported holiday season sales before reassessing the next quarter.
3. Refresh the leverage and liquidity view only from updated filing figures, keeping the internal figure on the total obligations basis that includes operating leases.
4. Continue closer monitoring on the filing basis while Harborline Retail remains on the watchlist, holding the existing position and making no new purchases.
