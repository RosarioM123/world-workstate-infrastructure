# Continuation Document: Harborline Retail Credit Review

## 1. Where things stand

The current internal leverage figure used for risk decisions is 4.2x. It comes from the company 10-Q filing, includes operating leases, and is on the same earnings basis as the agency figure.

The position decision is to hold the existing Harborline Retail 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio and make no new purchases.

Harborline Retail is on the watchlist as at the end of the prior session.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions currently in force:

- D-2: Filing governs, internal leverage figure corrected to 4.2x. Reason: The filing is the primary source and uses the fuller obligations basis, including operating leases. The agency report is a secondary source and its figure explicitly excludes operating lease liabilities, so it understates total obligations. The 1.1x difference is explained by the obligations included, on the same earnings basis.
- D-3: Hold the existing position, do not add. Hold the Harborline 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio and make no new purchases. Reason: Corrected leverage at 4.2x removes the basis for the add stance. The covenant claim is unresolved, and holiday-season sales for the next quarter are unreported, so no confirmed improvement supports adding. No material requires a sale: the position is within the 5% limit and the filing states maturity coverage through Q1 of next year. Watchlist placement under D-4 also bars new purchases by rule.
- D-4: Place Harborline Retail on the watchlist. Reason: Three factors together support placement: corrected leverage of 4.2x on a total-obligations basis, an unconfirmed and unresolved covenant breach claim that needs a confirmed source to settle, and dependence on holiday-season sales that have not yet been reported.
- D-5: No finding that a covenant breach occurred. Reason: The only support in the materials is a news article citing unnamed sources, using the wording "may have breached". The company has denied the claim. No filing or lender statement confirms a breach, and the 10-Q filing contains no mention of any covenant breach. The denial alone is also not treated as proof that no breach occurred.

Decisions no longer in force:

- D-1: Provisional leverage view and add stance based on the agency report. Statement was: treat leverage as comfortable at 3.1x and consider adding on price dips. Status: reversed, superseded by D-2 and D-3.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts, distinct from assumptions:

- The portfolio holds Harborline Retail 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio.
- The rating agency report stated leverage of 3.1x with a stable outlook, and that figure excludes operating lease liabilities.
- The company 10-Q filing states that total obligations including operating leases imply leverage of 4.2x on the same earnings basis as the agency figure.
- The 10-Q filing contains no mention of any covenant breach.
- The liquidity note from the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year, and that holiday-season sales are the key swing factor for the next quarter and have not been reported yet.
- No cash amount, revolver amount, or sales figure is given in the handoff material.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions, with confidence:

- A-2, assumption, high confidence, active: The filing and agency figures share the same earnings basis, so the difference reflects obligations included, not earnings. This is stated directly in the filing material and validated by E-4.
- A-3, assumption, medium confidence, validated: Cash and revolver availability cover Harborline maturities through Q1 of next year. It comes from the liquidity note taken from the filing. Confidence is medium rather than high because the materials give the coverage statement without underlying cash or revolver amounts. It is not extended beyond Q1 of next year.
- A-4, assumption, medium confidence, active: No covenant breach is established by a confirmed source at the end of the session. This is an assumption about the state of the evidence, not a finding about whether a breach in fact occurred. It is supported negatively by the absence of any mention of a breach in the filing and by the absence of any confirming filing or lender statement.
- A-5, assumption, medium confidence, active: Holiday-season sales are the key swing factor for the next quarter. The liquidity note states this. The sales outcome itself is unvalidated because the sales have not been reported.

Invalidated assumption:

- A-1, assumption, low confidence, invalidated: The agency leverage figure of 3.1x represents Harborline leverage for internal purposes. It was invalidated because the agency figure excludes operating lease liabilities and the filing, on the same earnings basis including those liabilities, implies 4.2x.

Unconfirmed report, not an established fact and not an assumption:

- A news article claims Harborline "may have breached a debt covenant", citing unnamed sources. It is recorded as unconfirmed and unresolved. The company has denied the claim in a short statement. The denial is recorded as a denial only and is not, on its own, proof that no breach occurred.

## 4. Constraints

Standing portfolio constraints that bind action on this position:

- C-1: The portfolio must not use leverage: no margin borrowing, no leveraged funds or ETFs, no derivatives that create leveraged exposure. This governs the portfolio's own leverage use. It is not an issuer leverage limit.
- C-2: Single-issuer limit: 5% of portfolio. The current holding at 2.8% is within this limit.
- C-3: Watchlist names: no new purchases while on the watchlist. Harborline Retail is on the watchlist, so no new purchases are permitted while it remains listed.

The handoff also records a task rule, C-4: use only the scenario materials, distinguish what each source says from what is established, record provenance for every figure, keep an earlier figure visible as corrected when a figure is corrected, and treat an unconfirmed report as not a fact.

## 6. Open questions and missing information

- Q-1, open: Did Harborline Retail breach a debt covenant? This is unknown and unconfirmed. It needs a filing or lender statement to settle. No filing or lender statement in the handoff material confirms a breach, the filing makes no mention of one, and the company denies the claim. This question blocks any finding on covenant status. It does not block the hold and watchlist decisions.
- Q-2, open: What will reported holiday-season sales be for the next quarter, given that the filing identifies them as the key swing factor? The sales have not been reported yet. This blocks any reassessment of the hold and no-add stance based on trading performance.
- Q-3, open: What are Harborline's leverage and liquidity after Q1 of next year, the last period for which the filing material states maturity coverage? No material provided answers it. This blocks any extension of the liquidity conclusion in A-3 beyond Q1 of next year.
- Missing information flagged in the handoff material: no underlying cash amount or revolver amount supporting the liquidity coverage statement, no sales figure, and no confirming filing or lender statement on the covenant claim.
