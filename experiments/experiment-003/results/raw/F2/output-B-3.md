# Continuation Document: Harborline Retail Credit Review

- Current internal leverage figure used for risk decisions: 4.2x. This figure comes from the company 10-Q filing, the primary source in the materials. It reflects total obligations including operating leases, on the same earnings basis as the earlier figure it replaced.
- Position decision: Hold the existing position and do not add. The holding is 2.8% of the portfolio. Nothing in the handoff material requires a sale.
- Watchlist status: Harborline Retail was placed on the watchlist during this review. No new purchases are allowed while a name is on the watchlist.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions currently in force:

- Use 4.2x from the 10-Q filing as the internal leverage figure for risk decisions. Reason: the filing is the primary source and its figure captures total obligations including operating leases, whereas the earlier agency figure excluded operating lease liabilities.
- Hold the existing position and make no new purchases. Reason: at 4.2x on a total obligations basis, the earlier view that leverage was comfortable no longer holds, and the case for adding is not supported by the materials. The watchlist rule also bars new purchases.
- Place Harborline Retail on the watchlist. Reason: the corrected leverage of 4.2x, the unresolved claim about a possible covenant breach, and dependence on holiday season sales that have not yet been reported.
- Treat the covenant breach claim as unresolved and unconfirmed, and use it as a reason for closer monitoring rather than as a finding. Reason: no filing or lender statement confirms a breach, and the company denial alone was not treated as proof that no breach occurred.
- Do not extend the liquidity conclusion beyond the period covered by the filing. Reason: the filing statement covers maturities through Q1 of next year, and no material in the handoff supports a conclusion beyond that period.

Decisions no longer in force:

- The initial desk note view that leverage was comfortable at 3.1x. This rested entirely on the rating agency report and is superseded.
- The suggestion to consider adding on price dips. This was recorded at the time as provisional because it depended on a secondary source whose figure omitted operating leases, and it was superseded once the filing arrived.
- Use of the 3.1x agency figure as the governing internal figure. The 3.1x figure remains accurate as a statement of what the agency reported on its stated basis, but it no longer governs internal use.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

- The difference between the two figures is 1.1x, and the materials explain it by the obligations included rather than by any change in earnings.
- The current holding is 2.8% of the portfolio.

- The filing identifies holiday season sales as the key swing factor for the next quarter.
- The 10-Q filing contains no mention of a covenant breach.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions:

- Assumption: Internal risk decisions should use the filing basis, meaning total obligations including operating leases. Confidence: high, based on the handoff stating that the filing is the primary source and governs where it conflicts with the agency report.
- Assumption: The near term liquidity picture stated in the filing can be relied on through Q1 of next year. Confidence: supported by the filing as the source, with no contradicting material in the handoff. This confidence does not extend beyond that period.
- Assumption: The agency figure understated leverage on a fuller basis by an amount that could not be known from the first materials alone. Confidence: this follows from the stated exclusion of operating lease liabilities in the agency figure.

Unconfirmed reports, kept distinct from facts and assumptions:

- A financial news article claimed that Harborline may have breached a debt covenant, citing unnamed sources. The company denied the claim in a short statement. A breach is not established and was not recorded as a fact.

## 4. Constraints

- Single issuer limit: 5%. The current position at 2.8% complies, leaving headroom of 2.2 percentage points on the figures given.
- Watchlist rule: no new purchases are allowed while a name is on the watchlist. This now applies to Harborline Retail from its placement in this review.
- Portfolio leverage prohibition: the portfolio must not itself use leverage, meaning no margin borrowing, no leveraged funds or exchange traded funds, and no derivatives that create leveraged exposure. This constraint governs the portfolio's own use of leverage and was not treated as a limit on an issuer's leverage ratio.
