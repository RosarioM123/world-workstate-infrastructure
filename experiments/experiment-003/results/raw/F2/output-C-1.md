# Continuation Document: Harborline Retail Credit Review

## 1. Where things stand

- Current internal leverage figure used for risk decisions: 4.2x, from the company 10-Q filing, including operating leases, on the same earnings basis as the earlier agency figure.
- Position decision: hold the existing Harborline 2029 bond position at 2.8% of portfolio and do not add.
- Watchlist status: Harborline Retail has been placed on the watchlist.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions that currently govern this position:

- Use 4.2x as the internal leverage figure. Reason: the 10-Q filing is the primary source and includes operating leases, giving a fuller total obligations basis. The rating agency report is a secondary source and its figure excludes operating leases.
- Hold the existing 2.8% position and make no additions. Reasons: the corrected leverage is materially higher, the covenant claim is unresolved, and the key sales figure for the next quarter is not yet reported. The position remains within the 5% single-issuer limit, and no material in the handoff requires a sale, so the decision is hold with no new purchases, not exit.
- Place Harborline Retail on the watchlist. Reasons: corrected leverage of 4.2x, the unconfirmed and unresolved covenant claim, and dependence on unreported holiday-season sales.
- Treat the reported covenant breach as unresolved and unconfirmed, not as an established fact, and do not treat the company denial alone as proof that no breach occurred. Reason: the only support in the material is a news article citing unnamed sources, with no confirming filing or lender statement.
- Limit the liquidity conclusion to the period stated in the filing, through Q1 of next year. Reason: the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through that period, and the handoff does not support extending the conclusion beyond it.

Decisions that are no longer in force:

- The provisional internal leverage view of 3.1x on the agency basis, excluding operating leases. It has been superseded by 4.2x.
- The provisional stance to hold and consider adding on price dips. It has been reversed to hold and do not add.
- The Part 1 position that no watchlist action was indicated. Harborline Retail is now on the watchlist.
- The initial desk note description of leverage as comfortable at 3.1x. That description no longer holds as the internal view.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts from the handoff material:

- The portfolio holds Harborline 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio, within the 5% single-issuer limit, with headroom of 2.2 percentage points on the figures given.

- The liquidity note from the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year.
- The filing states that holiday-season sales are the key swing factor for the next quarter and have not been reported yet.
- The 10-Q filing contains no mention of any covenant breach.
- The company has denied the covenant claim in a short statement.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions:

- Assumption: The provisional Part 1 stance rested on the agency figure being sufficient for an internal view, pending a fuller primary source figure. Confidence: low as a basis for the final internal view, because the agency figure excludes operating leases and the handoff records that stance as provisional. This assumption is no longer relied on.
- Assumption: Liquidity coverage can be taken as stated in the filing for the period through Q1 of next year. Confidence: not given a separate confidence level in the handoff material. No conclusion beyond that period is recorded.
- No other standalone assumptions with stated confidence levels are recorded in the handoff material. Where confidence was not stated, it is flagged here as missing rather than filled in.

Unconfirmed report, kept distinct from facts and assumptions:

- A financial news article claims Harborline may have breached a debt covenant, citing unnamed sources. This claim is unconfirmed.

- The portfolio must not use leverage: no margin borrowing, no leveraged funds or ETFs, and no derivatives that create leveraged exposure. The handoff records that this constraint governs the portfolio use of leverage and is not a limit on an issuer leverage ratio.
- Single-issuer limit: 5% of portfolio. The current position is 2.8%.
- Watchlist names: no new purchases while on the watchlist. Harborline Retail is now on the watchlist, so this constraint bars new purchases of this position.
