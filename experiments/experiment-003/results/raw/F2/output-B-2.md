# Continuation Document: Harborline Retail Credit Review

## 1. Where things stand

Current internal leverage figure used for risk decisions: 4.2x. This figure comes from the company 10-Q filing and reflects total obligations including operating leases.

Position decision: Hold the existing position and do not add. The holding is 2.8% of the portfolio. Nothing in the handoff material requires a sale.

Watchlist status: Harborline Retail was placed on the watchlist during this review.

## 2. Decisions in force

Decisions currently in force:

- Use 4.2x from the 10-Q filing as the internal leverage figure for risk decisions and monitoring. Reason: The filing is the primary source and captures total obligations including operating leases, on the same earnings basis as the earlier figure.
- Hold the existing position and make no new purchases. Reason: At 4.2x on a total obligations basis, the earlier case for adding is not supported, and nothing in the material requires an exit.
- Place Harborline Retail on the watchlist. Reason: The corrected leverage of 4.2x, the unresolved claim about a possible covenant breach, and dependence on holiday season sales that have not yet been reported.
- Treat the covenant breach claim as unresolved and unconfirmed, not as a fact. Reason: No filing or lender statement confirms a breach, and the company denial alone was not treated as proof that no breach occurred.
- Limit the liquidity conclusion to the period covered by the filing. Reason: The filing note covers maturities through Q1 of next year, and that conclusion was deliberately not extended beyond that period.

Decisions no longer in force:

- Use of 3.1x from the rating agency report as the working internal leverage figure. This figure excluded operating lease liabilities and has been corrected for internal use.
- Description of leverage as comfortable at 3.1x. This description no longer holds as the internal view at 4.2x.
- Provisional suggestion to consider adding on price dips. This stance was recorded as provisional, rested entirely on the agency report, and was superseded once the filing arrived.

The 3.1x figure remains accurate as a statement of what the agency reported on its stated basis, but it does not govern internal decisions.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

Established facts:

- The portfolio already holds Harborline Retail 2029 bonds, at 2.8% of the portfolio.
- The 10-Q filing reports leverage of 4.2x on a total obligations basis including operating leases.
- The rating agency report reported leverage of 3.1x with a stable outlook, and explicitly excluded operating lease liabilities.
- The difference between 4.2x and 3.1x is 1.1x, explained in the material by the obligations included rather than by any change in earnings. Both figures use the same earnings basis.
- A liquidity note taken from the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year. No other material contradicted that statement.
- The filing identifies holiday season sales as the key swing factor for the next quarter.
- Harborline Retail was placed on the watchlist during this review.

Assumptions, labeled as assumptions:

- Assumption: The filing basis, total obligations including operating leases, is the correct basis for internal monitoring. Confidence: High, based on the material stating that the filing is the primary source and that the agency basis understated leverage on a fuller basis.
- Assumption: Where the agency report and the filing conflict, the filing governs. Confidence: High, for the reason stated above.
- Assumption: The near term liquidity picture from the filing can be relied on for the period it covers, through Q1 of next year. Confidence: Supported by the filing and uncontradicted in the material, but not extended beyond that period.

Unconfirmed reports, distinct from facts and assumptions:

- A financial news article claimed Harborline may have breached a debt covenant, citing unnamed sources.
- The company denied the claim in a short statement.
- The 10-Q filing contains no mention of a covenant breach, and no filing or lender statement confirms a breach.
- Status of this claim: Unresolved and unconfirmed. It was not recorded as a fact.

## 4. Constraints

Standing portfolio constraints that bind action on this position:

- Single issuer limit of 5%. The current holding of 2.8% complies with this limit, leaving headroom of 2.2 percentage points on the figures given.
- Watchlist rule: No new purchases are allowed while a name is on the watchlist. This rule now applies to Harborline Retail from its placement in this review, so the decision not to add is barred by rule as well as by judgment.
- The portfolio must not itself use leverage. This means no margin borrowing, no leveraged funds or exchange traded funds, and no derivatives that create leveraged exposure. This constraint governs the portfolio use of leverage and was not treated as a limit on an issuer leverage ratio.
