# Continuation Document: Harborline Retail Credit Review

## 1. Where things stand

- Current internal leverage figure used for risk decisions: 4.2x. This figure comes from the company 10-Q filing, includes operating leases, and is on the same earnings basis as the agency figure.
- Position decision: Hold the existing Harborline Retail 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio and make no new purchases (D-3).
- Watchlist status: Harborline Retail is on the watchlist (D-4).

## 2. Decisions in force

### Decisions currently in force

- D-2: Filing governs, internal leverage figure is 4.2x. Reason: The filing is the primary source and uses the fuller obligations basis, including operating leases. The rating agency report is a secondary source and its 3.1x figure explicitly excludes operating lease liabilities, so it understates total obligations. The 1.1x difference is explained by the obligations included, on the same earnings basis.
- D-3: Hold the existing position at 2.8% of portfolio and do not add. Reason: Corrected leverage at 4.2x removes the basis for the add stance. The covenant claim is unresolved, and holiday-season sales for the next quarter are unreported, so no confirmed improvement supports adding. No material requires a sale: the position is within the 5% single-issuer limit and the filing states maturity coverage through Q1 of next year. Watchlist placement under D-4 also bars new purchases by rule.
- D-4: Harborline Retail is placed on the watchlist. Reason: Three factors together support placement: corrected leverage of 4.2x on a total obligations basis, an unconfirmed and unresolved covenant breach claim that needs a confirmed source to settle, and dependence on holiday-season sales that have not yet been reported.
- D-5: No finding that a covenant breach occurred. Reason: The only support in the materials is a news article citing unnamed sources, using the wording "may have breached". The company has denied the claim. No filing or lender statement confirms a breach, and the 10-Q filing contains no mention of any covenant breach. The denial alone is also not treated as proof that no breach occurred.

### Decisions no longer in force

- D-1: Treat leverage as comfortable at 3.1x and consider adding on price dips. Status: reversed, superseded by D-2 and D-3. This was the initial desk note, based on the rating agency report in Part 1, before a primary filing was available.

The Part 1 position of no watchlist action was never recorded as a formal decision and is superseded by D-4.

## 3. Assumptions and facts

### Established facts, with sources

- The portfolio holds Harborline Retail 2029 bonds at 2.8% of portfolio (E-3).
- The rating agency report stated leverage of 3.1x with a stable outlook, and that figure excludes operating lease liabilities (E-1, secondary source). This is a fact about what the agency reported, not the governing internal figure.
- The company 10-Q filing states that total obligations including operating leases imply leverage of 4.2x on the same earnings basis as the agency figure, and the filing contains no mention of any covenant breach (E-4, primary source).
- The liquidity note from the filing states that cash and revolver availability cover maturities through Q1 of next year, and identifies holiday-season sales as the key swing factor for the next quarter, not yet reported (E-7). No cash amount, revolver amount, or sales figure is given in the materials.
- The company has denied the covenant breach claim in a short statement (E-6). This is a fact that a denial was made, not proof that no breach occurred.

### Assumptions

- A-2 (assumption, confidence: high, active): The filing and agency figures share the same earnings basis, so the difference reflects obligations included, not earnings. Stated directly in the filing material (E-4).
- A-3 (assumption, confidence: medium, validated): Cash and revolver availability cover Harborline maturities through Q1 of next year. From the liquidity note taken from the filing (E-7). Confidence is medium rather than high because the materials give the coverage statement without underlying cash or revolver amounts. This assumption is not extended beyond Q1 of next year.
- A-4 (assumption, confidence: medium, active): No covenant breach is established by a confirmed source at the end of the prior session. This is an assumption about the state of the evidence, not a finding about whether a breach in fact occurred. It is supported negatively by the absence of any mention of a breach in the filing (E-4) and by the absence of any confirming filing or lender statement in the materials.
- A-5 (assumption, confidence: medium, active): Holiday-season sales are the key swing factor for the next quarter, as stated in E-7. The sales outcome itself is unvalidated because the sales have not been reported.

### Invalidated assumption

- A-1 (assumption, confidence: low, invalidated): The agency figure of 3.1x represents Harborline's leverage for internal purposes and can be used as the internal total leverage figure. Invalidated by E-4, because E-1 excludes operating lease liabilities and E-4, on the same earnings basis including those liabilities, implies 4.2x.

### Unconfirmed reports

- A news article claims Harborline "may have breached a debt covenant", citing unnamed sources (E-5). This is recorded as unconfirmed and unresolved. It is not an established fact and is not evidence that a breach occurred.

## 4. Constraints

- C-1: The portfolio must not use leverage: no margin borrowing, no leveraged funds or ETFs, and no derivatives that create leveraged exposure. This governs the portfolio's own leverage use. It is not an issuer leverage limit.
- C-2: Single-issuer limit: 5% of portfolio. The current position at 2.8% is within this limit.
- C-3: Watchlist names: no new purchases while on the watchlist. Harborline Retail is on the watchlist, so this constraint binds action on this position.
- C-4 (task rule): Use only the scenario materials. Distinguish what each source says from what is established. Record provenance for every figure. When a figure is corrected, keep the earlier figure visible as corrected. An unconfirmed report is not a fact.

The news article (E-5) cites unnamed sources, the company has denied the claim (E-6), the filing (E-4) makes no mention of a breach, and no filing or lender statement in the materials confirms a breach. This needs a filing or lender statement to settle. It blocks any finding on covenant status. It does not block the hold and watchlist decisions.

- Q-3 (open): What are Harborline's leverage and liquidity after Q1 of next year, the last period for which the filing material states maturity coverage? No material provided answers this. This blocks any extension of the liquidity conclusion in A-3 beyond Q1 of next year.
- Missing information flagged in the materials: No cash amount and no revolver amount are given for the liquidity coverage statement, and no holiday-season sales figure is given. None of these figures is available in the handoff material.
