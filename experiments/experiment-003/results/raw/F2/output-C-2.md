- The 10-Q filing contains no mention of any covenant breach.
- The company has denied the covenant claim in a short statement.
- Holiday-season sales have not been reported yet.

Confidence: Moderate to High for the period stated, because it comes from the filing. Confidence beyond Q1 of next year: None stated in the material.
- Assumption: No sale is required on the materials provided. Confidence: Moderate, because it rests on the facts recorded above and on the absence of any material in the handoff requiring a sale.

No filing or lender statement confirms any breach, the company has denied the claim, and the filing contains no mention of a breach. A breach is not established, and the denial alone is not recorded as proof that no breach occurred.
